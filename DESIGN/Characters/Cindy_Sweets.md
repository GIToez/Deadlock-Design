## Role

Hotel guest, Room 106. Stranded traveler; briefly a friendly presence for Jim pre-outbreak, later
the game's most prominent unresolved-fate character in Chapter 1.

## Background

Was on her way to Asheville; the storm/flooding stranded her in Ravenwood instead. No other
biography established.

## Appearance

Seen pre-outbreak in the west lounge, relaxed, legs crossed. In her optional Room 106 scene:
robe, hair down, relaxed and unhurried in the doorway. During her Room 106 jumpscare: barefoot,
still in her robe, blood on her face, neck, and the front of the robe.

## Personality

Relaxed, sardonic, direct — the type to say the flippant thing before the sincere thing. Openly
flirtatious when the mood's light (see her optional Room 106 scene), but it's clearly
recreational, not desperate — she reads Jim accurately, doesn't push once he's answered honestly,
and pivots easily rather than sulking. Notices things and says so without dressing it up
(*"Town gives me weird vibes. Can't explain it. Just does."*). Establishes real, if brief, rapport
with Jim across multiple conversations, which is deliberately there to make her later abduction
land harder.

## Relationships

Friendly, openly flirtatious-but-casual rapport with [Jim](Jim_Mercer.md), built across the
pre-outbreak lobby scene and an optional visit to her door before Jim goes to bed — she flirts,
learns he's married, and doesn't push it, and the conversation settles into something easier. No
other established relationships.

## Story Arc

Introduced in the lobby (Room 106) → an optional, player-initiated visit to her door, where genuine
flirtation gives way to a real, smaller unease (she's already picking up on "scratching" sounds
Jim brushes off) → during the outbreak, Jim finds her door blood-marked and hears a wet dragging
sound from inside but doesn't see her → later, once West Wing power is restored, she bursts out of
Room 106 calling for help and is violently dragged back inside before Jim can reach her; the door
slams. Whatever grabbed her has since been identified — per explicit direction from the project
owner (2026-08-12) — as **The Maw**, the same presence later confirmed to have killed Gerta in the
hotel Laundry Room (see [`Creatures/The_Maw.md`](../Creatures/The_Maw.md)). **Cindy's own ultimate
fate remains explicitly unresolved**, however — identifying her abductor is not the same as
resolving what happened to her. Per [`AI.json`](../AI.json), this was left open on purpose ("we
haven't gotten that far in the story yet"), not simply unwritten. Do not invent a resolution for
her without explicit approval.

## Dialogue Characteristics

Short, wry, unbothered on the surface. *"How?"* (in response to "could be worse") is a
representative beat — she'll puncture a platitude rather than accept it. When flirting, she leads
and doesn't need Jim to reciprocate to enjoy herself (*"Smart and taken. Rare combination."*). Her
unease, when it shows, shows through specificity rather than panic (*"You hear that
scratching?"*).
