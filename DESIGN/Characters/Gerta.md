# Gerta

## Role

Ravenwood Hotel night housekeeping staff. Briefly seen alive pre-outbreak; later found dead during
the outbreak, killed by **The Maw** — the first confirmed kill attributed to that creature.

> Note: originally logged as a "background NPC, no plot function" and not given a file. That
> changed once her death scene was approved (2026-08-12) — she now has a real, if small, story
> beat, so she gets a file per the "no random background NPCs" rule (she's no longer random).

## Background

Hotel night housekeeping staff. No further biography established.

## Appearance

Heavy-set, in a housekeeping uniform, pushing a loaded cleaning cart (towels, cleaning supplies, a
bucket hanging off one side).

## Personality

Barely characterized by design — her one pre-outbreak line ("Evenin'.") is deliberately ordinary,
the kind of non-interaction that makes a hotel feel staffed and lived-in without needing to be a
"character" yet. That ordinariness is what makes her death scene land the way it's meant to.

## Relationships

None established. A single, brief, wordless-except-for-a-greeting encounter with Jim.

## Story Arc

Glimpsed once, pre-outbreak, pushing her cart through the west hallway, then continuing on and
disappearing around a corner — a background beat, not a conversation. During the outbreak, Jim
finds her body in the hotel **Laundry Room** (a staff area reached by following a blood trail
through a staff storage door behind the hotel bar), killed by The Maw — the same presence that
dragged Cindy Sweets into Room 106 earlier in the chapter. Unlike Cindy, whose fate is left
explicitly open, Gerta's death is confirmed and shown on-screen (as a discovered aftermath, not a
witnessed kill).

## Important Scenes

- Pre-outbreak hallway glimpse — `Scripts/Chapter_1_One_Night_Only.md`, Scene 12.
- Death scene (the Laundry Room) — Scene 37.

## Dialogue Characteristics

Minimal by design. A single line of dialogue in the entire game (*"Evenin'."*), then none — she
never speaks again, including in her death scene, which plays entirely as a discovered aftermath
rather than a witnessed struggle.

## Established Facts

Killed by The Maw in the hotel Laundry Room. This is locked, per explicit direction from the
project owner (2026-08-12) — not to be changed without new explicit direction.
