> Reference portrait uploaded by the project owner (2026-08-13): reserved, professional look —
> glasses, blazer, books in hand — consistent with her established "quiet reader" characterization

> "normal" to distinguish from any future distressed/death-scene depiction, not yet portrayed.

## Role

Hotel guest, Room 112. Present throughout the pre-outbreak lobby scenes and the initial outbreak
panic; the game's first named on-screen death.

## Background

No biography established beyond being a quiet reader stranded at the hotel by the storm.

## Appearance

Seen reading near a hallway lamp, separate from the other guests, a book open across her knees.

## Personality

Reserved, dry, quietly observant. Doesn't look up from her book until she finishes the line she's
reading — a small but telling character beat (she's not going to be rushed, even by a stranger
talking to her). Her wit is understated (*"Depends how it ends."* / *"Haven't decided yet."*).

## Relationships

No established relationships beyond ordinary lobby proximity to the other guests. Meets
[Jim](Jim_Mercer.md) for the first time in the lobby.

## Story Arc

Introduced reading in the lobby → during the outbreak, found crouched and terrified outside her
room, refusing to go downstairs → later breaks and comes downstairs anyway when she sees Earl
attacking Jim, screaming for Jim to get away → runs for the front entrance during the chaos → **is
killed on-screen** when the crashing police cruiser pins her against a lobby pillar, permanently
blocking the hotel's front exit. Her death, and the image of her book left soaked in rainwater and
blood nearby, is meant to land as an earned loss — she was given enough voice beforehand that her
death isn't just a plot device.

## Important Scenes

- Lobby introduction / optional dialogue — [`Scripts/Chapter_1_One_Night_Only.md`](../Scripts/Chapter_1_One_Night_Only.md), Scenes 5, 8.
- Second Floor Hallway (found terrified) — Scene 19.
- First Combat (calls out from the stairs, then breaks and runs) — Scene 22.
- Police Cruiser Crash / her death — Scene 23.

## Dialogue Characteristics

Dry, minimal, deliberate — she doesn't waste words, which makes her later panic (*"What is wrong
with him—?!"*) land as a real break from her established composure rather than generic screaming.

## Established Facts

See [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md). Locked: dies in the police cruiser crash; her death permanently blocks the hotel's
front exit; this is the story's title-card beat ("Deadlock Protocol").

## Unresolved Ideas

None currently open — her arc is complete within Chapter 1.
