# Isolation Manual Page

> New item (2026-08-14), added when the Hospital's puzzle was rewritten to the Quarantine Puzzle —
> see [`CANON.md`](../../CANON.md) → "Five Puzzle Philosophies." Replaces the old "Laboratory Key"
> the dead Radiology orderly used to carry.

## Description

A torn, laminated page from the isolation system's own operating manual — a diagram of pressure
zones (Air Intake, Isolation Ward, Surgical Wing, Decontamination Corridor, Exhaust), half the
page torn away. Fits a matching gap in the Isolation Control Panel's own cover, and is enough,
even incomplete, to make sense of the panel's gauges.

## Story Placement

**St. Dymphna Hospital** (Chapter 2) — found on a dead orderly in Radiology, after the signature
transformed-patient encounter; required, alongside the [Backup Damper
Control](Backup_Damper_Control.md), to operate the Isolation Control Panel. See
[`Locations/Hospital.md`](../../Locations/Hospital.md) → "The Quarantine System" and "Key Items,"
and [`Scripts/Chapter_2_Hospital.md`](../../Scripts/Chapter_2_Hospital.md).
