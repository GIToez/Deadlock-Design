# The Founder's Token

> New item, locked 2026-08-14, replacing a purely mechanical "Jim needs four or more crests"
> gate on the Monastery's Sealed Passage / Old Caves with an actual in-world object and cause. See
> [`Locations/Memorial_Park.md`](../../Locations/Memorial_Park.md) → "The Founder's Token" and
> [`Locations/Monastery.md`](../../Locations/Monastery.md) → "The Sealed Passage / Old Caves."

## Description

A small, unlabeled brass token, its single edge worn into the shape of a keyhole rather than a
wedge — nothing like the five ceremonial crests. Drops free from a hidden mechanism behind the
Founders Memorial's own FAITH recess the first time Jim returns with any four of the five crests
already inserted into the medallion, regardless of which four. Abbot Matthias Kane — a Memorial
founder and the Monastery's own 1887 charterer — built his own recess to react this way as a
personal failsafe: proof the rest of Ravenwood's institutions could be trusted before his own
order's deepest secret was ever exposed.

## Story Placement

**Memorial Park** (Chapter 2) — obtained from the Founders Memorial once any four crests are
inserted. Opens the Monastery's Sealed Passage / Old Caves — a previously-invisible lock at its
threshold, beneath the ancient warning inscriptions. Not required for the Faith Crest itself, only
for the Monastery's final, deepest scene (Brother Cormac's last broadcast); the Faith Crest can
still be collected at any time regardless of when — or whether — Jim has the token yet.

## Story Significance

Gives the Monastery's crest-count-gated finale an actual physical cause rather than an abstract
inventory check: nothing mystical happens when Jim reaches four crests, and the door doesn't
recognize how full his pockets are. What changes is that a hidden 1887 mechanism — built by one of
the five founders specifically to verify the other four institutions before revealing what his own
had sealed — finally releases something. The Monastery itself doesn't change at all until Jim
physically brings the token there.
