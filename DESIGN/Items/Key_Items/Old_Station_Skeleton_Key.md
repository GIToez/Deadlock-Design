## Description

An old skeleton key, kept in the Chief's Office desk specifically because the old 1887 station
house has historically been the Chief's responsibility, not the desk sergeant's. Opens the
breezeway door into the old station house — a specific, single-purpose key rather than a generic
workaround.

## Story Placement

**Police Station** (Chapter 2) — found in the Chief's Office (opened with the
[Chief's Office Key](Chiefs_Office_Key.md)); opens the breezeway connecting the modern annex to
the original 1887 station house. See
[`Locations/Police_Station.md`](../../Locations/Police_Station.md) → "Key Items."
