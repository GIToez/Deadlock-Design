## Description

A brass key, taken from Earl Whitaker's body after the lobby fight. Opens the staff hallway leading
to the Manager's Office.

## Story Placement

**Ravenwood Hotel** (Chapter 1) — taken from Earl Whitaker's body; opens the Staff Hallway to the
Manager's Office (the chapter's first safe room). See
[`Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md`](../../Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md) → "Key Items."
