## Description

A small mechanical override control, hospital-issue, left behind mid-shift-change in an abandoned
physician's office at the Medical Arts Building — a deliberate cross-location backtrack away from
the main hospital building entirely. Seats into the isolation control panel's own damper slot back
at St. Dymphna Hospital, completing what the torn isolation manual page (found in Radiology) alone
can't finish.
