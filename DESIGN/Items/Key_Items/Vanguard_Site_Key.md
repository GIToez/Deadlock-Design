## Description

A dark, corporate-issue keycard found at the Loading Docks, alongside shipment manifests confirming
years of unmarked refrigerated transport trucks. Opens a locked filing cabinet *inside* the
Vanguard Site Office (2026-08-14, revised — the office itself is unlocked, per the Casting Puzzle
rewrite; this key now gates only a bonus-lore cabinet, not the room or anything the casting line
needs).
