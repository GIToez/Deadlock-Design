## Description

A small laminated card — orange and white — bearing the Ravenwood Emergency Management seal.
Printed below the seal: "STREET INFRASTRUCTURE OVERRIDE / SECTOR 4 — MEMORIAL PARK PERIMETER."
Lowers the street bollards blocking Memorial Park's south gate, opening Ravenwood's open-world
exploration phase.
