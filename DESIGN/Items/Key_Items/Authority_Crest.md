## Description

A bronze, wedge-shaped medallion bearing a relief portrait of Marshal Josiah Hale, his name and
title, and a small relief of a skeleton key. Kept in a dusty glass display case in the old station
house's main hall; breaking the case (old, brittle glass) retrieves it.
