# Key Items

Keys, tools, and unique story-critical objects that gate a specific door/puzzle/reward. See
[`../README.md`](../README.md) for the overall Items convention, concept-art rules, and the
"Reusable key/keycard icon templates" note (important context before assuming a shared icon means
two entries are the same item).

## Current files

**Ravenwood Hotel (Chapter 1):**

- [`Managers_Key.md`](Managers_Key.md) — from Earl Whitaker's body; opens the Staff Hallway/
  Manager's Office.
- [`Housekeeping_Closet_Key.md`](Housekeeping_Closet_Key.md) — from Fennimore's body; opens the
  Housekeeping Closet.
- [`Screwdriver.md`](Screwdriver.md) — Utility Room; opens the breaker panel's fuse housing. Has a
  real in-game sprite (`spr_screwDriver.png`).
- [`Auxiliary_Fuse.md`](Auxiliary_Fuse.md) — West Wing Maintenance Room; restores West Wing power.
  Has a real in-game sprite (`spr_fuseItem.gif`).
- [`Gate_Crank_Handle.md`](Gate_Crank_Handle.md) — Housekeeping Closet; manual override for the
  courtyard's north gate.

**Police Station (Chapter 2) — puzzle: the Lockdown Puzzle (see [`CANON.md`](../../CANON.md) →
"Five Puzzle Philosophies"):**

- [`Chiefs_Office_Key.md`](Chiefs_Office_Key.md) — from Sergeant Calloway; opens the Chief's
  Office, where the lockdown assignment order is found.
- [`Old_Station_Skeleton_Key.md`](Old_Station_Skeleton_Key.md) — Chief's Office; the physical
  latch under the Breezeway's electronic shutter (Zone 4), once the Armory's panel resets it.
- [`Armory_Zone_Override.md`](Armory_Zone_Override.md) — from Corporal Reyes' body (renamed from
  "Armory Key," 2026-08-14, once the district's puzzle became the Lockdown Puzzle); resets the
  Armory's lockdown (Zone 3) and, from inside, the Breezeway's control panel.
- [`Evidence_Room_Key.md`](Evidence_Room_Key.md) — optional; a padlocked locker inside the
  Property & Evidence Room; opens the Courthouse's Clerk's Exhibit Storage.
- [`Bolt_Cutters.md`](Bolt_Cutters.md) — optional; Fire Station; opens the padlocked locker inside
  the Property & Evidence Room (not the room's own door, which resets electronically).
- [`Pocketknife.md`](Pocketknife.md) — optional, Booking & Processing; no confirmed mechanical use
  yet.
- [`Authority_Crest.md`](Authority_Crest.md) — the Southwest District's founder's emblem; old
  station house display case.

**Memorial Park (start of Chapter 2):**

- [`Bollard_Override_Keycard.md`](Bollard_Override_Keycard.md) — guardhouse desk; lowers the
  street bollards, opening the open-world exploration phase. Has a real in-game sprite
  (`spr_keycard.png`).
- [`Founders_Token.md`](Founders_Token.md) — new 2026-08-14; drops from the Founders Memorial's
  FAITH recess once any four crests are inserted; opens the Monastery's Sealed Passage / Old
  Caves. Replaces a purely mechanical crest-count gate with an actual physical cause.

**St. Dymphna Hospital (Chapter 2) — puzzle: the Quarantine Puzzle:**

- [`Fire_Axe.md`](Fire_Axe.md) — Ambulance Bay; general-purpose forcing tool, no confirmed
  puzzle-gating use yet.
- [`Isolation_Manual_Page.md`](Isolation_Manual_Page.md) — from a dead orderly in Radiology (new
  2026-08-14, replaces "Laboratory Key" — the Laboratory is now unlocked); one of two components
  needed to operate the Isolation Control Panel.
- [`Backup_Damper_Control.md`](Backup_Damper_Control.md) — Medical Arts Building (secondary
  location; renamed from "Surgical Wing Access Card," 2026-08-14); the other component needed at
  the panel.
- [`Pry_Bar.md`](Pry_Bar.md) — optional, Vanguard Quarantine Checkpoint (secondary location);
  forces the barricaded Psychiatric Ward stairwell.
- [`Medical_Crest.md`](Medical_Crest.md) — the Northeast District's founder's emblem; St. Dymphna
  Chapel display case, unlocked once the ICU is passed — no key of its own.

  *Retired 2026-08-14 (rooms now unlocked, no replacement item needed): "Laboratory Key,"
  "Administration Key," "Chapel Key."*

**Steelgate Refinery (Chapter 2) — puzzle: the Casting Puzzle:**

- [`Restricted_Elevator_Access_Card.md`](Restricted_Elevator_Access_Card.md) — the (unlocked)
  Manager's Office; opens the Restricted Elevator, the district's one remaining "real" gate on the
  critical path.
- [`Vanguard_Site_Key.md`](Vanguard_Site_Key.md) — optional; Loading Docks (secondary location);

- [`Research_Bay_Key.md`](Research_Bay_Key.md) — the Exposure Records Room; opens the Research Bay.
- [`Cutting_Torch.md`](Cutting_Torch.md) — Machine Shops (secondary location); forces the Security

- [`Boardroom_Key.md`](Boardroom_Key.md) — dropped after clearing the boss fight against The
  Foreman and the Exposure Cohort; opens the Founder's Boardroom.
- [`Industry_Crest.md`](Industry_Crest.md) — the Northwest District's founder's emblem; freed from
  the seized mold carriage by the Casting Line puzzle, at the Casting Hall — not the Founder's
  Boardroom (revised 2026-08-14; the Boardroom's own display case is now empty).

  *Retired 2026-08-14 (room now unlocked, no replacement item needed): "Manager's Office Key."*

**Worthy Academy (Chapter 2) — puzzle: the Archive Puzzle:**

- [`Administration_Office_Key.md`](Administration_Office_Key.md) — optional; the Cafeteria; opens

- [`Isolation_Wing_Key.md`](Isolation_Wing_Key.md) — optional; the Administration Office; opens

**Our Lady of Solace Monastery (Chapter 2) — puzzle: the Bell Tower Puzzle, added 2026-08-14:**

- [`Archive_Key.md`](Archive_Key.md) — optional; Overlook Trail (secondary location); opens the
  Hidden Archive.
- [`Crypt_Antechamber_Key.md`](Crypt_Antechamber_Key.md) — optional; Hillside Cemetery (secondary
  location); opens the Crypt Antechamber (a dead end by design, not a shortcut).
- [`Faith_Crest.md`](Faith_Crest.md) — the North District's founder's emblem; the Reliquary,
  reached once the Bell Tower Puzzle's altar seal is solved and The Penitent is defeated — no key
  of its own. **Completing this district completes all five crests.**

  This district's critical path is almost entirely key-free by design — see
  [`Locations/Monastery.md`](../../Locations/Monastery.md) → "The Bell Tower."
