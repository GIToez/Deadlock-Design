## Description

A bronze, wedge-shaped medallion, the last of the five, found in the Reliquary beyond The Penitent's
chamber — no key of its own, reached once the Bell Tower Puzzle's altar seal is solved and the Old
Seal / Crypt Depths pack encounter and boss are cleared. A relief portrait of a hooded, bearded man
fills most of it, with his name, title, and a cross emblem cast beneath him:

> **ABBOT MATTHIAS KANE**
> **FOUNDER**
> **1887**

## Story Placement

**Our Lady of Solace Monastery** (Chapter 2) — the North District's founder's emblem; the
Reliquary, reached once the crypt beyond the Old Seal is cleared. Completing this district
completes all five crests — the Founders Memorial's water basin can then be drained, revealing the
staircase down to Chapter 3. See [`Locations/Monastery.md`](../../Locations/Monastery.md) → "Key
Items" and "Crest Progression," and
[`Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md`](../../Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md).
