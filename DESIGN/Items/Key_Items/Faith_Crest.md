# Faith Crest

> One of the five founders' crests — see [`CANON.md`](../../CANON.md) → "The Founders & the Five
> Crests." The Monastery is fully open-order like the other four districts, so Faith can be Jim's
> first crest just as easily as his last; nothing about it is inherently "final."

## Description

A bronze, wedge-shaped medallion, found in the Reliquary beyond The Penitent's chamber — no key of
its own, reached once the Bell Tower Puzzle's altar seal is solved and the Old Seal / Crypt Depths
pack encounter and boss are cleared. A relief portrait of a hooded, bearded man fills most of it,
with his name, title, and a cross emblem cast beneath him:

> **ABBOT MATTHIAS KANE**
> **FOUNDER**
> **1887**

## Story Placement

**Our Lady of Solace Monastery** (Chapter 2) — the North District's founder's emblem; the
Reliquary, reached once the crypt beyond the Old Seal is cleared. Only once **all five** crests
(from all five districts, collected in any order) are inserted into the Founders Memorial does its
water basin drain, revealing the staircase down to Chapter 3. See
[`Locations/Monastery.md`](../../Locations/Monastery.md) → "Key Items" and "Crest Progression," and
[`Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md`](../../Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md).
