## Description

A security-zone control fob, still clipped to Corporal Eli Reyes' duty belt. Resets the Armory's
own lockdown (Zone 3) from an interior control point, and — from inside the Armory — the
Breezeway's control panel (Zone 4), the post Reyes was heading to when he stopped to check on
Diesel and Baxter.

## Story Placement

**Police Station** (Chapter 2) — from Corporal Eli Reyes' body, K-9 Unit Room (found immediately
after surviving the Alpha Ashen Hound boss fight); resets the Armory's lockdown, where the
[Ranger 870 Pump Shotgun](../../Weapons/Ranger_870_Pump_Shotgun.md) and
[Shotgun Shells](../Consumables/Shotgun_Shells.md) are recovered, and — from inside — resets the
Breezeway's own lockdown next. See
[`Locations/Police_Station.md`](../../Locations/Police_Station.md) → "The Lockdown System" and
"Key Items," and [`Scripts/Chapter_2_Police_Station.md`](../../Scripts/Chapter_2_Police_Station.md).
