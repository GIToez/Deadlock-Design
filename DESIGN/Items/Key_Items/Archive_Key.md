# Archive Key

> New item writeup (2026-08-14) — previously referenced in

## Description

A plain door key, found on a dead monk along the Overlook Trail — he didn't make it far, documents
scattered around him. Opens the Hidden Archive, Brother Cormac's own secret collection, off the
Library/Archive.

## Story Placement

**Our Lady of Solace Monastery** (Chapter 2, optional) — found on the Overlook Trail (secondary
location); opens the Hidden Archive. A pure lore reward — not required for the Faith Crest, and
doesn't shortcut the Bell Tower Puzzle. See
[`Locations/Monastery.md`](../../Locations/Monastery.md) → "Key Items" and "Puzzles," and
[`Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md`](../../Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md).
