## Description

A plain key, tagged "ANTECHAMBER," found near a very recent unmarked grave at the Hillside
Cemetery — a shovel dropped rather than put away beside it. Opens the Crypt Antechamber, where
Vanguard's own failed attempt to drill past the Bell Tower's mechanism is still visible.

## Story Placement

**Our Lady of Solace Monastery** (Chapter 2, optional) — found at the Hillside Cemetery (secondary
location); opens the Crypt Antechamber. A pure lore reward — proof Vanguard tried and failed to
bypass the bell mechanism with modern drilling equipment, a dead end by design rather than a
shortcut. Not required for the Faith Crest. See
[`Locations/Monastery.md`](../../Locations/Monastery.md) → "Key Items" and "Puzzles," and
[`Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md`](../../Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md).
