# Vanguard Access Card

![Vanguard Access Card — concept icon](../../Assets/Reference/item_vanguard_access_card_concept.png)

*AI-generated inventory-icon concept (2026-08-13) — deliberately distinct from the generic
municipal `spr_keycard.png` template (see [`../README.md`](../README.md) → "Reusable key/keycard
icon templates"): sleek, dark corporate design rather than the orange/white Ravenwood Emergency
Management card, visually signaling it doesn't belong to the town's own infrastructure.*

## Description

A duplicate/stolen Vanguard Public Safety Liaison access card, found hidden in Detective Aaron
Cole's desk or ceiling tiles alongside his abandoned investigation. Opens every door in the
station bearing Vanguard's own hidden footprint — doors no ordinary Ravenwood PD key ever could.

## Story Placement

**Police Station** (Chapter 2, optional Vanguard sub-plot — see
[`CANON.md`](../../CANON.md) → "Vanguard's Grip on Ravenwood PD"). Found in
[Detective Aaron Cole's office](../../Locations/Police_Station.md). Opens the Vanguard Liaison
Office, the Cold Cells (via the Basement Stairwell), and the hidden Vanguard Observation Booth
behind the Interview Room. Entirely optional — does not gate the Authority Crest or any part of
the mandatory critical path.
