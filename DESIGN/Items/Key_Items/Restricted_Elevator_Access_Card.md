## Description

A plant-issue access card, left deliberately on Plant Manager Daniel Fitch's own desk once he
decided to stop protecting Vanguard's access to the underground levels. Opens the Restricted
Elevator.

## Story Placement

**Steelgate Refinery** (Chapter 2) — found in the Plant Manager's Office; opens the Restricted
Elevator down into the Vanguard-controlled underground levels. See
[`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](../../Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md) → "Key Items" and
"Puzzles."
