## Description

A bronze, wedge-shaped medallion bearing a relief portrait of Eleanor Worthy, her name and title,
and a torch emblem. Kept in a glass display case in Founder's Hall, deep within the main Academy
building; the case opens without needing to be forced.

symbol — see [`CANON.md`](../../CANON.md) → "The Founders & the Five Crests"). Found in Founder's
