## Description

A bronze, wedge-shaped medallion bearing a relief portrait of Eleanor Worthy, her name and title,
and a torch emblem. Kept in Principal Ashford's hidden archive, behind the Library's own historical
timeline display — not Founder's Hall's display case, which Ashford emptied and left a note in
during the evacuation (revised 2026-08-14, once the Archive Puzzle replaced the district's old key
chain).

## Story Placement

**Worthy Academy** (Chapter 2) — the Southeast District's founder's emblem (KNOWLEDGE wedge, Torch
symbol — see [`CANON.md`](../../CANON.md) → "The Founders & the Five Crests"). Found in the
Library's hidden archive, revealed once the Archive Puzzle's chronology is solved and the five
founder plaques are placed in order. Can be returned to the Founders Memorial any time Jim
backtracks to Memorial Park. See [`Locations/Academy.md`](../../Locations/Academy.md) → "The
Archive" and "Crest Progression."
