## Description

A small key, taken from the body of Fennimore, the hotel's night-shift security guard, in the
courtyard. Opens the Housekeeping Closet on the guest floor's east hallway.

## Story Placement

**Ravenwood Hotel** (Chapter 1) — taken from Fennimore's body in the courtyard (Scene 42); opens
the Housekeeping Closet between Rooms 114 and 116 (Scene 43), source of the Gate Crank Handle. See
[`Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md`](../../Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md) → "Key Items" and
[`Characters/Fennimore.md`](../../Characters/Fennimore.md).
