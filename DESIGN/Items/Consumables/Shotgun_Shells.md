# Shotgun Shells

> Ammo for the [Ranger 870 Pump Shotgun](../../Weapons/Ranger_870_Pump_Shotgun.md).

![Shotgun Shells — in-game sprite](spr_shotgunAmmo.png)

*Real in-game sprite, uploaded by the project owner (2026-08-13).*

## Description

A box of 12-gauge shells, compatible with the Ranger 870. Recovered alongside the shotgun itself,
plus additional stock elsewhere in the district.

## Story Placement

**Police Station** (Chapter 2) — twelve shells recovered with the shotgun in the Armory, plus
additional ammo in the Break Room (optional). See
[`Locations/Police_Station.md`](../../Locations/Police_Station.md) → "Key Items."
