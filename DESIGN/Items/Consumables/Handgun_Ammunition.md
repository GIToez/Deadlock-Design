# Handgun Ammunition

> Ammo for the [Sentinel-9 Service Pistol](../../Weapons/Sentinel_9_Service_Pistol.md).

![Handgun Ammunition — in-game sprite](spr_handgunAmmo.png)

*Real in-game sprite, uploaded by the project owner (2026-08-13).*

## Description

A small box of 9mm rounds, compatible with the Sentinel-9 Service Pistol. Found before the handgun
itself, which is a deliberate genre-standard pattern (a stockpile ready for when the gun arrives).

## Story Placement

**Ravenwood Hotel** (Chapter 1) — three caches plus what's taken directly from Officer Pruitt's
body: the East Wing Utility Room supply drawer (Scene 32), the West Wing Staff Room locker (Scene
37), and the Courtyard Security Office desk drawer (Scene 40). See
[`Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md`](../../Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md) → "Key Items."
