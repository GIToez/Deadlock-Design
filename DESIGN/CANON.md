## Fundamental Premise

Deadlock Protocol follows **Jim Mercer**, a field infrastructure engineer traveling through
Appalachia during a severe storm. After becoming stranded, Jim checks into the **Ravenwood
Hotel** in the town of Ravenwood for what he believes will be a single overnight stay. The town
has already begun collapsing due to an outbreak, and the game escalates from grounded, slow-burn
horror into full quarantine horror.

*Source: `Deadlock Protocol - Story Design Rebuild.docx`, TAB 01 — Core Story Overview.*

## Origin of the Outbreak

The collapse of Ravenwood is tied to an **underground Vanguard BioSystems outbreak** connected to
**"Project Ashen."** The specific mechanism/cause of the outbreak beyond this attribution is not
yet established — see `STORY_NOTES.md`.

*Source: `Deadlock Protocol - Story Design Rebuild.docx`, TAB 01 — Core Story Overview.*

## Major Character Relationships

- Jim Mercer is in a committed relationship with **Sarah Mercer** ("love you" is exchanged on
  both sides of every call). Sarah is off-site for the events of the game and reachable only by
  phone during the prologue; she loses signal contact with Jim once he checks into the hotel.
  (Exact relationship status — married vs. partnered — is not explicitly stated in the source
  material; see `STORY_NOTES.md`.)

*Source: `Deadlock Protocol - Story Design Rebuild.docx`, Scenes P1–P3, L-series.*

## Major Story Structure

The game opens with a grounded, slow-burn **Prologue** (driving into Ravenwood, checking into the
hotel, a quiet first night) before escalating into full outbreak horror once **"Deadlock
Protocol"** is declared over police radio. The first full chapter of the game takes place almost
entirely within the **Ravenwood Hotel** and its courtyard, ending with the player's first exit
into the streets of Ravenwood proper. See `MASTER_STORY.md` for the full progression and
`Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md` for complete hotel-chapter detail.

*Source: `Deadlock Protocol - Story Design Rebuild.docx`, TABs 01–12 (body numbering).*

## Locked Facts

The source document explicitly marks the following as `STATUS: FINALIZED CANON — LOCKED`
(covering the Prologue and the Hotel Lobby Introduction only — see `STORY_NOTES.md` for the
open question on whether later hotel material carries the same lock status):

- Heavy rainstorm arrival on Highway 13.
- Jim Mercer arrives in Ravenwood via Jeep Wrangler.
- The Ravenwood Hotel neon sign reads "RAVENWOOD HOTEL — EST. 1956 — VACANCY."
- Optional parking-lot interactions occur before entering the hotel.
- **Earl Whitaker** (hotel night clerk) is introduced at check-in, and gives Jim Room 104.
- The first infected reveal occurs in the hotel lobby: Earl Whitaker is shown feeding on a guest.
- A crashing police cruiser blocks the hotel's front exit (and kills guest Janeth Caldwell in the
  process).
- The "Deadlock Protocol" radio announcement is the game's title-drop, declaring the outbreak
  quarantine.
- Jim acquires a baseball bat before acquiring any firearm.
