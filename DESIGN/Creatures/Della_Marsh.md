# Della Marsh (the Red Room Singer)

## Concept

An optional mini-boss encounter designed to feel "surreal, dreamlike, deeply uncomfortable" —
distinct in tone from the chaos-driven horror everywhere else in the hotel. Unlike most of Chapter
1's threats, she's introduced ambiguously: the player may initially mistake her for a survivor
before the details resolve into something wrong.

## Origin

**Della Marsh** — stage name "Della M." — a local Ravenwood performer with a four-year weekly
residency at the Ravenwood Hotel's Red Room lounge (per the hotel's own booking records: Friday
and Saturday nights, two sets, well-liked by guests and staff). Infected and killed sometime during
the outbreak's early hours, while still performing or shortly after. Not connected to Vanguard in
any way established so far — an ordinary local caught in the outbreak, like Roy Bullock and the
rest of the hotel's staff and guests.

## Appearance

Seated at a spotlit microphone on the Red Room's small stage, microphone still in hand, head
slightly bowed, blood running down the mic stand in thin lines. On approach: gray skin, a dark
stain across the front of her dress, her throat visibly wrong. Once she turns: dead white eyes,
blood across her mouth, an expression that "starts as a smile and keeps going past where smiles
stop."

## Behavior

Passive and unaware-seeming at first — she continues singing quietly to herself, barely audible
under the Red Room's damaged, skipping jazz loop, surrounded by the slumped bodies of an audience
that never left. She stops the moment Jim gets close, turns toward him, and rises to attack. The
encounter plays out through the same dim red lighting and looping distorted music throughout — no
lighting/atmosphere shift for combat, which is part of what makes it feel different from the rest
of the hotel's more chaotic encounters.

## Gameplay Role

Optional mini-boss, entirely self-contained within the Red Room (which does not connect to
anywhere else in the hotel — see [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) → "Retcons"). Skippable entirely; rewards exploration
with optional supplies and two documents (a booking slip and a personal, unfinished note to her
mother — full text in [`Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md`](../Locations/Ravenwood_Hotel.md)) that are the only place her ordinary,
pre-infection humanity is shown on the page.

## Encounter Progression

Single, one-off encounter — not a repeating enemy type.

## Major Appearances

- [`Scripts/Chapter_1_One_Night_Only.md`](../Scripts/Chapter_1_One_Night_Only.md), Scene 38.

## Story Significance

Mostly atmospheric/optional — a mood beat rather than a plot beat. Her personal note (written as
she began feeling unwell mid-shift, trying to call her mother, being told by Earl to stay inside
without explanation) is a small, human-scale window into how quietly and quickly the outbreak
turned inside the hotel, told from someone who never got to understand what was happening to her.
