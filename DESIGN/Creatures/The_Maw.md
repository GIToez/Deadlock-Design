# The Maw

## Concept

A stalking, ambush-type infected threat that stays largely **unseen** through Chapter 1 — the
game's first taste of a predator the player can't yet fully look at, let alone fight. Where the
Caretaker is a confrontational, telegraphed boss fight, The Maw is the opposite: fast, grabby,
glimpsed only in fragments, and gone before the player can process what happened.

## Origin

Not yet established. Presumed to be a Black Vein infection case, like everything else encountered
in Ravenwood, but its specific origin (who it was before infection, if that's even knowable) is
`_TBD_`. Unlike Roy Bullock (the Caretaker) or Della Marsh, no pre-infection identity has been
confirmed for The Maw.

## Appearance

**Deliberately unclear as of Chapter 1.** Every encounter with it so far is a partial, fast
glimpse rather than a clear look:

- Grabbing Cindy Sweets in Room 106: *"Something from inside the room grabs her. Fast — too
  fast — almost too fast to properly see."*
- At Gerta's death (the Laundry Room): not seen directly at all — only a receding, wet, low sound
  moving away through the wall, and a wound pattern Jim notes doesn't match anything he's fought
  so far ("less like teeth and hands, more like something closed around her all at once").

This is intentional — do not invent a full physical description without explicit approval. The
name itself ("The Maw") is the strongest hint given so far about its nature.

## Behavior

Ambush-based rather than pursuit-based, in contrast to both the Caretaker (deliberate, stalking,
pursues openly) and standard shamblers (wander, then close in once they notice the player). The
Maw strikes fast from concealment (inside a room, from beyond a wall) and withdraws immediately —
it has not been shown lingering to fight. Both known incidents happen in enclosed, staff-adjacent
spaces of the West Wing (a guest room; a laundry room reached via a staff corridor), which may or
may not be meaningful — not yet confirmed as a pattern.

## Gameplay Role

Currently non-combat — an atmosphere/dread creature rather than an encounter the player fights in
Chapter 1. Both of its confirmed incidents are aftermath/jumpscare beats (Cindy's abduction; Gerta's
body). Whether it becomes a fightable enemy or boss later in the game is undecided.

## Major Appearances

- `Scripts/Chapter_1_One_Night_Only.md`, Scenes 35 and 37.

## Story Significance

Establishes that not every threat in Ravenwood is a shambler or a named, sympathetic infected
character — something is out there that the player (and Jim) can't yet see clearly or understand,
which is a deliberate tonal shift from the rest of Chapter 1's more legible threats. Cindy Sweets'
ultimate fate remains a separate open question (see `Characters/Cindy_Sweets.md` and
`STORY_NOTES.md`) — confirming The Maw as her abductor does not, on its own, confirm whether she's
alive, dead, or something in between.

## Open Design Gaps

- Full appearance/physical design.
- Whether it recurs beyond Chapter 1, and if so, where and how (fightable enemy? recurring dread
  beat? tied to a specific location like the sewers or a later district?).
- Whether "Maw" is its full name or shorthand for something with more lore behind it later.
