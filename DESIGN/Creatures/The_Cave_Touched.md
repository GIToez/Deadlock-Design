# The Cave-Touched

> New creature, locked 2026-08-14, per [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) → "Creature Distribution
> System." Every district needs a standard-tier new creature distinct from its boss, one that
> unlocks into the city-wide roaming-hazard system once discovered — the Monastery's own version
> of the Ashen Hound (Police Station), the Broodling (Hospital), and the Exposure Cohort
> (Foundry). Previously an open question in
> [`Locations/Monastery.md`](../Locations/Monastery.md); given a full write-up here rather than
> staying as an inline description only.

**Classification:** Ashen Mutant, direct-exposure variant (distinct from every other creature's
origin story — not a bite, not a Vanguard facility, just proximity to exposed Black Vein itself)
**Encounter Type:** Pack/signature encounter (multiple individuals, same structural role as the
Ashen Hound pair and the Exposure Cohort)
**Known Population:** an unspecified number of people who wandered into the wider cave network
over an unknown span of time — not a single incident, not a monitored cohort

## Concept

Every other infected creature in the game can be traced to a specific cause: a bite, chronic
industrial exposure, prolonged medical trauma, a night spent restrained next to something
worsening. The Cave-Touched have none of that. They're what happens when a person simply walks
too far into the cave system beneath Ravenwood and spends enough time near exposed Black Vein
itself, with no intermediary at all. Where the Exposure Cohort reads as *settled/adapted* from
years of monitored, quantified exposure, the Cave-Touched should read as *quietly wrong* — calm,
unhurried, mineral-streaked, the visible early version of what centuries of the same exposure
eventually made of [The Penitent](The_Penitent.md).

## Origin

Per [`Locations/Monastery.md`](../Locations/Monastery.md) → "Outbreak Night," beat 21, the old
seal beneath the monastery — already compromised by Vanguard's own illicit tunnel from the Foundry
side — opened the lower chambers to the wider cave network on Outbreak Night. The Cave-Touched are
among whatever reached the monastery basement through that route: not Foundry workers, not
Vanguard personnel, just people (and, per the same underlying exposure mechanism established for
the Ashen Hound, potentially anyone or anything else that spent time in those tunnels) who
encountered Black Vein directly, with no institution or event in between. Their exact number and
identities are deliberately unspecified — nobody at the monastery has any record of who they were.

## Appearance

Faint mineral-streaked discoloration beneath the skin — the same dark, branching Black Vein visual
motif established for the Ashen Hound, here appearing as a much earlier, subtler stage than The
Penitent's centuries-advanced version. Clothing varies (they were never a monitored group, so no
shared uniform or coverall like the Exposure Cohort) but should read as ordinary and unremarkable —
whoever they were before, nothing marks them as belonging to any particular institution.

## Behavior

Unusually calm and settled in movement, closer to deliberate walking than a Shambler's stiff
lurch or an Exposure Cohort member's coordinated stalking — proposed as the mechanical bridge
between the Shambler baseline and The Penitent's own slow, weighted inevitability. Encountered as
a small group approaching the Old Seal / Crypt Depths, per
[`Locations/Monastery.md`](../Locations/Monastery.md) → "Storyline."

## Gameplay Role

The Monastery's own contribution to the district structure's now-standard shape: a pack encounter
distinct from the district's boss, guarding the approach to [The Penitent](The_Penitent.md)'s
chamber and the Reliquary beyond it. See
[`Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md`](../Scripts/Chapter_2_Monastery.md), Scene 9.

**Also this district's contribution to the city-wide creature unlock system** (see
[`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) → "Creature Distribution System"): once Jim survives the approach to the
Old Seal, standard-tier Cave-Touched can begin appearing as a roaming hazard on Ravenwood's open
streets, scaling with crest count same as every other district's creature.

## Story Significance

Where every other district's signature creature is legible as a specific institution's failure —
police procedure, chronic industrial monitoring, medical trauma — the Cave-Touched have no
institution behind them at all. They're proof that Black Vein doesn't need Vanguard, doesn't need
an industrial accident, doesn't need a hospital's malpractice to hurt someone; it just needs
proximity. This reinforces the Monastery's whole thematic point (see
[`Locations/Monastery.md`](../Locations/Monastery.md) → "Purpose in the Overall Story") that
Vanguard didn't invent this danger — it's been here, doing this, long before any of Ravenwood's
institutions existed to fail people with it.

## Open Design Gaps

- No formal combat spec (health, attack patterns, exact speed/range values) — same gap flagged for
  every other creature's own file.
- Exact pack size for the Old Seal approach not yet decided.
- No concept art yet.
- Whether any Cave-Touched individual should ever be identified (a name, a reason they were in the
  caves) or stay permanently anonymous — leaning toward permanently anonymous, consistent with how
  the Exposure Cohort is also kept as an unnamed group rather than individual character arcs.
