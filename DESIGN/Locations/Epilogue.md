## Purpose in the Overall Story

The epilogue is deliberately brief — a coda, not a chapter. Its entire job is to be a clean sequel
hook: hand the story off to **[Sarah Mercer](../Characters/Sarah_Mercer.md)** without resolving
Jim's fate, and make the player want to follow her into a next game without needing it explained.
Per [`AI.json`](../AI.json): *"It doesn't explain the next game. It doesn't undercut the ambiguity
of Jim's ending. It simply introduces the next protagonist in the most emotionally loaded way
possible."* The player already knows and cares about Sarah — she's been a voice on the phone since
Scene 1 of Chapter 1 — so by the time she appears here, no introduction is needed.

## Arrival / Setup

- Some amount of time has passed since the end of Chapter 3 (exact duration not yet decided — see
  Unresolved below). Sarah has received Jim's one outbound message from the Vanguard facility (see
  [`MASTER_STORY.md`](../MASTER_STORY.md) → Chapter 3) and has come to Ravenwood herself.
- She arrives at the outskirts of the town and reaches the **quarantine perimeter** — the same
  Deadlock Protocol lockdown that sealed Ravenwood off at the start of Chapter 1.
- She goes through it anyway.

## Storyline

- **The Approach.** Sarah arriving at the edge of Ravenwood; the quarantine perimeter as a visible,
  physical barrier (not yet described in further detail — likely echoes the checkpoint imagery
  already established elsewhere, e.g. the Hospital district's own quarantine checkpoint, but this
  is not confirmed).
- **Through the Perimeter.** She doesn't hesitate or turn back. No internal monologue or dialogue
  is specified yet — the beat is meant to play as quiet and resolute rather than explained.
- **The Dark City Ahead.** Ravenwood is shown from her point of view for the first time — dark,
  unresolved, still whatever it became by the end of Chapter 3.
- **Closing Image.** Per [`AI.json`](../AI.json): *"Sarah's silhouette moving through the rain
  toward the city."* No resolution of Jim's fate. No reunion. No answer given.
