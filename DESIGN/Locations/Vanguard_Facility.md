## Purpose in the Overall Story

The endgame location. This is where Jim (and the player) learns the full truth behind Ravenwood's
outbreak — Project Ashen, Black Vein, and Vanguard BioSystems — and where the story resolves (with
Jim's ultimate fate left deliberately ambiguous). Reached via a staircase revealed beneath the
Founders Memorial statue in Memorial Park once all five emblems are returned.
