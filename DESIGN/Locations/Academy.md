# Worthy Academy

> The Southeast District's main location — Knowledge Crest. Everything below is new (2026-08-13,
> proposed pending review), written to the same [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) → "District Main-
> Location Design Standard" bar as [`Locations/Police_Station.md`](Police_Station.md),
> [`Locations/Hospital.md`](Hospital.md), and [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](Foundry_Refinery.md):
> a dense, multi-key, backtracking-driven exploration space, targeting roughly 2–3 hours of
> gameplay.
>
> **Thematic identity (per the project owner's explicit direction):** where the Foundry, Hospital,
> and Police Station each show a Ravenwood *institution* discovering Vanguard's betrayal, the
> Academy shows **ordinary civilians** who did everything right — went to the designated shelter,
> listened to police, registered their families, waited for evacuation — and were failed anyway,
> because Vanguard had already spent years quietly compromising every institution they trusted.
> *"The Academy represents the final failure of Ravenwood's emergency system."* Its environmental
> storytelling is deliberately **personal** rather than institutional: handwritten rosters, teacher
> notes, children's drawings, family photographs, PA announcements, lost-and-found items — not
> machinery, medical charts, or dispatch logs.
>
> **Structural note:** [`STORY_NOTES.md`](../STORY_NOTES.md)'s earlier city-layout table listed
> "Academy Library" as one of this district's secondary locations, alongside Student Housing,
> Athletic Field, and Playgrounds. Per the source material below (the campus's own library is one
> of its core rooms, and becomes "The Library Group" survivor pocket), the library is treated here
> as **part of the main Academy building**, not a separate off-campus location — a minor
> structural reconciliation, not a contradiction; nothing about its content changes, only where
> it's categorized. Student Housing, Athletic Field, and Playgrounds remain this district's three
> secondary locations.

## Purpose in the Overall Story

The Southeast District's main "mansion/RPD"-style location, following the same structural rules as
the Police Station, Hospital, and Foundry: a central hub (the Gymnasium) visibly gates several
locked doors at once, and reaching the **Knowledge Crest** requires a genuine multi-key
backtracking loop across the main campus building and three secondary locations. Where the other
three institutions each fail because Vanguard corrupted them from within, Worthy Academy fails
because it did exactly what a civilian shelter is supposed to do — and Vanguard used that
cooperation against it, turning the shelter's own registry into a tool for locating exposure
cohorts and their families.

## Outbreak Night — What Actually Happened (proposed 2026-08-13, pending review)

> Per the project owner's explicit framing: *"If the Foundry is where Ravenwood's disaster begins,
> the Academy should be where the town tries to save its children and families... It is about what
> happens when hundreds of terrified civilians gather in one place while nobody actually
> understands what they're hiding from."* This timeline is the master reference behind the
> specific details placed in `Storyline`, below.

1. **Before the outbreak, the Academy is Ravenwood's designated emergency shelter** — storm-tested,
   equipped with backup generators, emergency food and water, cots, first aid, and radios; the
   police have used it for previous weather emergencies. Nobody imagined it would need to shelter
   the town from itself.
2. **The storm warning begins.** An ordinary evening — basketball practice, drama rehearsal,
   custodial staff, tutoring, students waiting for pickup — as the same storm hitting the rest of
   Ravenwood worsens. Police contact the Academy: **PREPARE FOR POSSIBLE EMERGENCY SHELTER
   ACTIVATION.** Staff open the gymnasium, still believing this is weather plus an industrial
   accident.
3. **The first civilians arrive** — not fleeing monsters, fleeing practical problems: car
   accidents, power outages, flooded streets, an apartment fire, families who can't reach
   relatives. Police direct people here because the hospital is already filling. Staff set up
   registration tables, cots, food stations, a children's area, and basic triage. For a short
   time, the Academy may be the safest place in Ravenwood.
4. **The first Vanguard instruction arrives indirectly through police**: anyone showing
   industrial-incident symptoms (fever, confusion, unexplained bleeding, violent behavior, severe
   muscle spasms, unusual wound healing) must be separated from the general population. Untrained
   teachers, secretaries, coaches, and custodians convert the nurse's office and adjacent
   classrooms into an isolation area, not yet understanding what that decision will do.
5. **Police bring the first Foundry families.** Workers who escaped early reach residential areas;
   their families are evacuated here, and some Foundry employees arrive themselves — one covered
   in mineral dust, one with a badly injured arm, one insisting he's fine. Shelter staff separate
   them per Vanguard's instructions; a woman refuses to leave her husband in isolation; someone
   shouts that Vanguard is lying. See [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](Foundry_Refinery.md), beat
   19.
6. **Police presence is initially reassuring** — familiar Ravenwood officers, not an anonymous
   tactical unit, controlling entrances, calming civilians, and establishing one arrival entrance,
   one emergency-vehicle exit, a temporary missing-person board, secured supply rooms, and campus
   patrols.
7. **Vanguard requests the complete shelter roster** — names, addresses, medical conditions,
   employment, hospitalization history, with Foundry employees specifically flagged. Administration
   assumes it's for evacuation planning. One teacher notices Vanguard's real interest: not
   necessarily the sick, but people connected to Foundry workers or already on Vanguard's lists —
   the shelter's own registry becomes how Vanguard locates its exposure cohorts.
8. **A family is removed.** Vanguard personnel take custody of a Foundry worker, his wife, and
   their teenage son — neither the wife nor son is sick. The guarding officer asks why; Vanguard
   says *"Potential secondary contamination."* He reluctantly allows it. His later note: *"I gave
   them the Harris family." "All three."*
9. **The Academy fills rapidly** — hospital overflow, evacuated residential blocks, stranded
   traffic-accident families; eventually hundreds of people across the gymnasium, cafeteria, and
   classrooms. Large boards go up: **INSIDE / HOSPITAL / POLICE / MISSING / UNKNOWN** — names
   crossed out, moved, annotated, some simply left under MISSING.
10. **The first person turns inside the shelter** — not someone obviously infected, but a Foundry
    employee who passed screening, suffered a minor injury hours earlier, and collapses in the
    cafeteria. Someone performs CPR; his body reacts violently; he attacks the person helping him.
    Panic, overturned tables, a stampede toward exits, police shooting, and now everyone
    understands the danger is already inside.
11. **The police lock the doors** — unable to let hundreds of panicked civilians flood the
    streets, but from the civilians' side, indistinguishable from being imprisoned. *"LET US OUT."*
    One officer, aloud, over the radio: *"Jesus Christ. We're doing exactly what they did."*
    Deliberately mirrors [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](Foundry_Refinery.md)'s own Vanguard
    lockdown.
12. **The school is divided into zones** — **GREEN** (Gymnasium, asymptomatic), **YELLOW**
    (classrooms, uncertain exposure), **RED** (Nurse's Office/science wing, symptomatic). Initially
    life-saving, then horrific: families separated, children taken from symptomatic parents, people
    lying during screening (a mother hiding a wound, a father dosing a child with fever medicine)
    once rumors spread that Vanguard is taking people away.
13. **The hospital tells them the truth.** A transmission from St. Dymphna warns that traumatic
    injury may accelerate abnormal regeneration and to avoid invasive treatment unless absolutely
    necessary — useless, terrifying advice for people running a shelter. The Academy nurse writes:
    *"Everything I was trained to do may be making them worse."* See
    [`Locations/Hospital.md`](Hospital.md), beat 6.
14. **Police are ordered to reinforce Highway 13.** Half the Academy's security detail is pulled
    away — *"We've got two hundred people here." "I know."* — into the Police Station's own
    highway confrontation (see [`Locations/Police_Station.md`](Police_Station.md), beat 9). Some
    never return. The Academy's defenses immediately weaken.
15. **The PA system becomes the lifeline** — escalating announcements from "please remain calm" to
    "do not enter the east hallway" to "if you hear gunfire, stay inside your room and lock the
    door." Fragments of these can still play from damaged speakers when Jim arrives.
16. **Vanguard demands all RED-zone civilians be surrendered.** The remaining officers, having
    heard what happens to people Vanguard takes, hesitate; one refuses outright. Vanguard: *"You
    are interfering with a federal containment operation."* Asked for legal authority, they can't
    or won't provide it — the first time Ravenwood Police physically blocks Vanguard from a
    civilian shelter.
17. **The standoff outside the Academy** — Vanguard vehicles arrive, police barricade the front
    entrance, civilians watch through windows. Nobody fires initially; some officers and Vanguard
    guards know each other personally from prior emergency drills. This is the Academy's version of
    the Foundry's Loading Yard confrontation and the Hospital's Ambulance Bay firefight.
18. **The chief's broadcast reaches the Academy.** *"All surviving officers disregard Vanguard
    containment orders."* An officer locks the front doors and tells Vanguard, *"You heard him."*
    One of the rare moments the player can feel proud of Ravenwood — and already too late. See
    [`Locations/Police_Station.md`](Police_Station.md), beat 14.
19. **An evacuation is planned** — the shelter can't hold indefinitely (too many people, not
    enough food or medical supplies, possible infected inside). Buses are prepared, passenger lists
    made, children assigned to adults, a police escort planned, potentially routing toward Memorial
    Park.
20. **The roads collapse before the escort arrives.** Police units are killed or trapped at
    Highway 13; a bus sent ahead never reports back; dispatch eventually stops responding entirely.
    The Academy doesn't know RPD headquarters is itself collapsing (see
    [`Locations/Police_Station.md`](Police_Station.md), beats 10–15).
21. **The isolated Yellow Zone deteriorates** — concealed symptoms and indirect exposure catch up;
    someone attacks another person; the infection reaches the hallway; fire doors seal the east
    academic wing with people trapped on the wrong side.
22. **Someone opens the doors.** A father whose daughter is trapped in the quarantined wing begs,
    is refused, punches an officer, steals the access keys, and opens the fire door. Some
    survivors escape; so do infected. A human mistake, not a villainous one — and it helps destroy
    the shelter.
23. **Panic in the gymnasium** — hundreds try to leave at once; the barricaded front entrance
    (because of Vanguard) forces everyone toward packed side exits; children are separated from
    parents; police fire on approaching creatures; the organized shelter disintegrates in minutes.
24. **Vanguard abandons the Academy** once it becomes uncontrollable — not defeated, just no
    longer worth the risk. The remaining police realize: the people Vanguard called too dangerous
    to release weren't dangerous enough for Vanguard to stay and help contain. That tells them
    everything.
25. **The Academy fractures into survivor pockets** rather than falling all at once: the **Gym
    Survivors** (families, remaining officers), the **Library Group** (teachers, younger
    students), the **Auditorium Group** (originally preparing for evacuation), the fully
    compromised **Science Wing**, **Administration** (a handful maintaining communications), and
    the **Maintenance Basement** (custodians keeping backup power running).
26. **The last school bus.** Thirty-two civilians load aboard with an RPD volunteer escort; the
    front gates open; dispatch logs *"Bus One departing with thirty-two,"* then later,
    unanswered, *"Bus One, report." "Bus One?"* Static. Jim may find this bus elsewhere in
    Ravenwood later — a deliberately open side story.
27. **The children left behind.** During the chaos, teachers write student names on classroom
    boards: **PICKED UP / WITH PARENT / SHELTERED / MISSING.** A teacher's notebook: *"Do not tell
    the younger ones the police are gone." "They keep asking when their parents are coming." "I
    don't know what to tell them anymore."*
28. **The final Academy broadcast** — Principal Diane Ashford, on the emergency radio: *"This is
    Ravenwood Academy emergency shelter. We have civilians remaining inside. We have children
    here." "Any Ravenwood unit receiving, please respond."* Nothing. *"Anybody?"* Static. This
    transmission is recorded at the Police Station after most officers are already dead — nobody
    ever answers it.
29. **The last defensive stand.** Remaining officers and adults barricade a major hallway while
    teachers and custodians move children through a maintenance corridor toward an escape route.
    One officer leaves a note with his badge: *"If they make it out, that's enough."* Whether that
    group survives is deliberately left open, discoverable later rather than answered here.

## Arrival / Setup

- Worthy Academy should initially look strangely intact from outside — a deliberate contrast with
  the Foundry and Hospital. School banners still hang, emergency lights glow, a basketball
  scoreboard sits frozen mid-game, student artwork lines the outer walls. The barricaded front
  entrance and abandoned Vanguard-standoff positions are the only immediate signs anything is
  wrong.
- That contrast breaks the moment Jim enters the Gymnasium: hundreds of abandoned cots, blankets,
  food trays, children's backpacks, missing-person boards, and police barricades. This was supposed
  to be the safe place.
- Fragments of Principal Ashford's PA announcements can still intermittently play from damaged
  speakers throughout the building, escalating in urgency the deeper Jim goes.

## Characters Encountered

- **Principal Diane Ashford** — never seen alive or dead; known entirely through her office, the
  PA recordings, and her final broadcast. Her ultimate fate is deliberately unresolved, same
  convention as Daniel Fitch and the Police Station's Chief.
- The Harris family, the Auditorium Group, the Library Group, and the last defensive stand's
  officer are all referenced through documents/environmental storytelling only — per the project
  owner's explicit direction that this location's horror is told through people rather than a
  single named antagonist or survivor.

## Creatures Encountered

- **[Shamblers](../Creatures/Shambler.md)** only — the Cafeteria's single signature encounter and
  the East Academic Wing's pack encounter. No new creature type is introduced for this district
  (see "The East Academic Wing / Science Rooms," above, and "Unresolved Ideas," below) — a
  deliberate choice consistent with the project owner's framing that the Academy's horror is about
  people, not a new monster.

## Puzzles

- **The Gymnasium hub.** Four visibly locked/barricaded points (Administration Office, Auditorium,
  Library, Maintenance Basement) are all seen at once before any of them can be opened — same "see
  the shape of what's left to solve" convention as the other three main locations.
- **The Cafeteria → Administration → Isolation Wing key chain.** A straightforward single-purpose
  lock-and-key sequence revealing the Vanguard registry/Harris family material.
- **The Student Housing → Auditorium → Library chain.** The Auditorium Key can't be found inside
  the main building at all — a deliberate district-wide backtrack, mirroring the Foundry's Loading
  Docks and the Hospital's Medical Arts Building conventions. The Library Key is then found inside
  the Auditorium itself, a short internal follow-up step.
- **The Athletic Field → Maintenance Basement → East Wing chain.** Generator Fuel is likewise found
  at a separate secondary location, and the Fire Axe recovered in the Basement is needed to force
  the East Wing's jammed doors — the second deliberate district-wide backtrack.
- **The East Wing → Principal's Office → Founder's Hall → Escape Corridor chain.** The Knowledge
  Crest, and the Maintenance Escape Corridor beyond it, are only reachable after clearing the
  district's signature pack encounter, keeping the emblem — and the location's emotional climax —
  behind the deepest, most-gated point, per the design standard.

Faith Crest) or backtrack to Memorial Park.

## Unresolved Ideas

- **This entire district is a first draft, pending review** — same status the Foundry, Hospital,
  and (originally) the Police Station had before their own reviews/restructures were approved.
- None of the nine new key items above have individual `Items/Key_Items/` writeups yet — proposed
  as a future pass alongside the Foundry's and Hospital's own still-pending `Items/` audits.
- Whether Bus One's fate (and the fate of the last defensive stand's escape group through the
  Maintenance Escape Corridor) should ever be resolved elsewhere in the game, or remain permanently
  ambiguous — explicitly left open per the source material's own framing ("whether that escape
  group succeeds doesn't have to be answered immediately... it can become something Jim discovers
  later").
- No unique named boss creature or new creature type is proposed for this district (see "Boss
  Encounters" and "Creatures Encountered," above) — deliberate, per the project owner's framing,
  but flagged in case that's reconsidered later for pacing reasons (every other Chapter 2 main
  location has at least a proposed signature creature beyond standard Shamblers).
- Whether Principal Diane Ashford deserves her own dedicated `Characters/` file (matching Aaron
  Cole's and Daniel Fitch's treatment) rather than being described only within this location file —
  not done here, consistent with how the Police Station's Chief and the Hospital's Vanguard
  Liaison were also left undocumented outside their own location files.
- Full scene-by-scene scripting into a `Scripts/` file — everything above is at the
  location-design/prose level, matching where the Foundry and Hospital stood before their own
  (still-pending) scripting passes.
- The Harris family — introduced here purely as an environmental-storytelling detail (a name on a
  note) — could be expanded into a fuller subplot later if useful, but is not proposed as a named
  `Characters/` entry at this time.
