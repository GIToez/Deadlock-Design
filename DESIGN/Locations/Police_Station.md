# Ravenwood Police Station

> [`Scripts/Chapter_2_Ravenwood.md`](../Scripts/Chapter_2_Ravenwood.md), Scenes 22–33 (district
> entry through the three secondary locations). [`AI.json`](../AI.json) only reached outline-level
> detail for this district — the interior of the station itself, its survivor, and the emblem's

## Purpose in the Overall Story

The natural first of the five open-order districts — closest to Memorial Park, and the explicit
destination the guardhouse groundskeeper's note points toward ("look at the police station... the
old building... not the annex"). Delivers the game's first returned emblem (the **Authority
Crest**), its second firearm (a shotgun), and its first Tier 2 conditional survivor
([Sergeant Ruth Calloway](../Characters/Ruth_Calloway.md)) — the mechanic locked in
[`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) but not yet given a concrete example until now.

## Arrival / Setup

- Jim leaves Downtown Ravenwood (after City Hall) and crosses into the Southwest civic complex —
  visually distinct from downtown: institutional architecture, wider streets, chain-link, the
  department's own failed emergency response (crashed/abandoned cruisers, a scattered barricade)
  visible before Jim ever reaches the building itself.
- The station is two connected structures: a **modern annex** (the working police department —
  bullpen, armory, dispatch) and, separated by a breezeway, the **original 1887 station house** — a
  small stone building, now boarded up and used only for historical display, that the modern
  department has stopped thinking about. The groundskeeper's note's distinction ("not the annex —
  the original 1890s structure") is the reason this split exists.

## Storyline

- **The Lobby.** A bulletproof-glass reception window (intact) traps a lone shambler behind it — a
  deliberate non-threat, meant to be seen and bypassed rather than fought.
- **The Bullpen.** One shambler encounter. An intact evidence locker yields the **Evidence Room
  Key** (no use here — it's a cross-location reward for the City Courthouse, Scene 33). Sergeant
  Calloway's voice is heard from the barricaded Records/Dispatch room at the back.
- **Sergeant Calloway.** If the station is visited first, she's alive, barricaded in
  Records/Dispatch. She confirms Deadlock Protocol's scope (sealed at the county line, not just
  Ravenwood), gives Jim the **Armory Key**, and — asked directly — confirms she's seen "the old key
  thing" in the old station house's display case and that it bears the name **Marshal Josiah
  Hale**, the department's first peace officer. She stays behind. If a different district is
  visited first, she doesn't survive; see her character file for the (not yet scripted)
  alternate version of this beat.
- **The Armory.** The armory key opens a room of mostly-emptied gun racks; one still holds a
  clamped-down shotgun and two boxes of shells, freed with the screwdriver from Jim's own bag. The
  district's key equipment reward.
- **The Breezeway / Navigation Puzzle.** The direct connecting door into the old station house is
  locked from the annex side with an old-fashioned deadbolt — a dead end. Jim instead exits through
  the breezeway's exterior door and works his way around outside to a boarded-up front entrance
  that's already been partially pried open by someone else, at some earlier point, for reasons
  never explained.
- **The Old Station House.** A single hushed room, stone walls, a wall of every chief/marshal's
  photograph going back to 1887. A dusty glass display case holds the **Authority Crest** — a
  bronze, wedge-shaped medallion bearing a relief portrait of Marshal Josiah Hale, his name and
  title, and a small relief of a skeleton key. Breaking the case (old, brittle glass) retrieves it.
  This is the direct payoff of the Memorial Park guardhouse note.
- **The Old Holding Cells.** A small optional area behind the main hall — disused cells repurposed
  for records storage. A damaged 1887 town-charter document lists Hale among five "Incorporators of
  Ravenwood," the other four names illegible from water damage — a deliberate, honest way to defer
  naming the remaining four founders until their districts are written.
- **Secondary Locations.** The **Fire Station** (lore/timeline via a dispatch call-sheet board that
  cuts off mid-sentence, plus supplies), the **Municipal Garage / Impound Lot** (supplies, a
  navigation shortcut gate, and the district's signature encounter — two Ashen Hounds, mutated K-9
  unit dogs, the game's first fast/pack-hunting enemy type), and the **City Courthouse** (an
  unresolved survivor-camp environmental beat, plus a locked evidence room openable with the
  bullpen's Evidence Room Key).

## Important Rooms / Areas

**Modern Annex:**
- Lobby (reception window, a bypassable shambler)
- Bullpen (evidence lockers, tactical map, Evidence Room Key)
- Records / Dispatch (Sergeant Calloway)
- Armory (locked; shotgun + shells)
- Breezeway (connects annex to the old station house; direct door is a dead end)

**Original 1887 Station House:**
- Main Hall (photograph wall; the Authority Crest display case)
- Old Holding Cells (optional; records storage; damaged founding document; optional Medkit)

**Secondary Locations (district, not the main building):**
- Ravenwood Fire Station
- Municipal Garage / Impound Lot (K-9 transport van; Ashen Hound encounter; shortcut gate)
- City Courthouse (courtroom; jury deliberation room; locked evidence room)

## Characters Encountered

## Creatures Encountered

- **[Shamblers](../Creatures/Shambler.md)** — lobby (behind glass, bypassable) and bullpen (one

## Puzzles

- **Evidence Room Key → City Courthouse.** Found in the station's bullpen, used two locations
  later at the Courthouse — a deliberate cross-district reward loop.
- **Armory Key.** Given by Sergeant Calloway (or, in the alternate path, recovered from her body)
  rather than found cold — gates the shotgun behind an earned trust/tragedy beat instead of a blind
  pickup.
- **The Breezeway Navigation Puzzle.** The obvious direct route between the two buildings is a dead
  end; the real route is an exterior workaround — a small, grounded "solve it by looking at your
  environment" puzzle per [`AI.json`](../AI.json)'s original city-design philosophy.

## Key Items

- **Evidence Room Key** — bullpen evidence locker; opens the City Courthouse's evidence room.
- **Armory Key** — from Sergeant Calloway (or her body); opens the armory.
- **Shotgun** + **Shotgun Shells x12** — armory.
- **Authority Crest** — the district's founder's emblem; old station house display case.

### Documents

- Evidence bag tag (bullpen) — flavor only, explains the Evidence Room Key's origin.
- Marshal Josiah Hale's display-case nameplate (old station house).
- Damaged 1887 town-charter page listing five "Incorporators of Ravenwood" (old holding cells;
  only Hale's name is legible).
- Fire Station dispatch call-sheet board (cuts off mid-call, night of the outbreak).
- Municipal Garage mechanic's maintenance log / personal note (birthday cake reminder, never used).
- Courthouse judge's-bench case file (an ordinary pre-outbreak property dispute).

## Major Scripted Events

- Sergeant Calloway's rescue-or-discovery beat (conditional on visit order).
- Retrieving the shotgun from the armory.
- The breezeway/old-building navigation puzzle.
- Breaking the display case and retrieving the Authority Crest — direct payoff of the Memorial
  Park guardhouse note.
- The two-Ashen-Hound encounter at the Municipal Garage.

## Boss Encounters

No full boss fight in this district (contrast with Chapter 1's Caretaker). The Ashen Hound pair at
the Municipal Garage functions as the district's "major encounter" per
[`AI.json`](../AI.json)'s per-district design rule, without escalating to boss-fight scale.

## Crest Progression

Source of the **Authority Crest** (Southwest / ORDER wedge / Key symbol — see
[`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) → "The Founders & the Five Crests"). Can be returned to the Founders
Memorial any time Jim backtracks to Memorial Park; not forced immediately after pickup.

## Exit / Progression to Next Area

Once the Authority Crest and shotgun are collected and the three secondary locations explored, Jim
is free to head to any of the remaining four districts (Hospital, Academy, Refinery, Monastery, in
any order) — not yet scripted.
