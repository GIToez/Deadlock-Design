# Chapter 2 — Our Lady of Solace Monastery

> Scene-by-scene script for the North District — Faith Crest. Written from scratch (2026-08-14),
> the fifth and final district to receive this treatment — the only one with no prior script to
> supersede, since [`Locations/Monastery.md`](../Locations/Monastery.md)'s **Bell Tower Puzzle**
> was designed as this district's mechanic from the start rather than a retrofit. See
> [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) → "Five Puzzle Philosophies."
>
> **Jim's arc note:** the Monastery is fully open-order, mechanically identical to the other four
> districts — no special gate, no dependency on Memorial Park, no assumption about visit order (see
> [`Locations/Monastery.md`](../Locations/Monastery.md)'s header note for the history of two earlier
> versions that tried to force it into a "last district" role, both reverted). Write Jim's actual
> dialogue using his real crest count at the time per
> [`Characters/Jim_Mercer.md`](../Characters/Jim_Mercer.md) — this script's own branches (the Faith
> Crest pickup at Scene 11, conditioned on whether it's actually his fifth crest) are the model:
> check the real number, never assume it. The Sealed Passage (Scene 12) is reachable as soon as
> the Faith Crest is in hand, with no separate condition of its own.
>
> Brother Cormac is never seen alive or dead in this script, per his location file's convention —
> everything about him is reconstructed from the Hidden Archive, the Cloister, and the final
> broadcast.

---

## SCENE 1 — NORTH DISTRICT ENTRY / APPROACH

Jim leaves whichever part of Ravenwood he was in behind. The road climbs — switchbacks up the
ridge, the storm somehow louder up here, the city's lights shrinking below him with every turn. For
the first time tonight, the sound of gunfire and alarms fades rather than gets closer.

Then, faint, carried on the wind: a bell. Not ringing now — just an echo of one that already rang,
hours ago.

### Crest-count branch (per [`Characters/Jim_Mercer.md`](../Characters/Jim_Mercer.md))

**0 crests (Survival):**

> **JIM:** *"...Somebody's still up there."*

**1–2 crests (Suspicion / Pattern):**

> **JIM:** *"...Wonder what this one's been hiding."*

**3+ crests (Anger and past it):**

Jim doesn't say anything. He just keeps climbing.

## SCENE 2 — MONASTERY GATE / APPROACH

![Monastery Gate / Approach — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_gate_approach_concept.png)

The gate stands open. A Vanguard-funded floodlight, mounted awkwardly onto centuries-old stonework,
hangs dead on its bracket. Beyond it: the monastery itself, low stone buildings around a courtyard,
a single lit window somewhere upstairs.

*Interaction prompt: [APPROACH]*

Compared to everywhere else tonight, it's quiet. Almost peaceful. Jim doesn't trust it.

---

## SCENE 3 — THE CHAPEL

![Chapel — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_chapel_concept.png)

Pews pushed aside for makeshift cots, candles burned down to stubs. Beneath a centuries-old side
staircase: a Vanguard decontamination station, sleek and out of place against the stone.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE STATION]*

> **JIM:** *"...Even here."*

A faded mural above the altar shows four robed figures, each standing beneath a different bell:
**Dawn. Labor. Mourning. Rest.**

*ITEM NOTED: MURAL — Bell Tower clue 1 of 3 (see Scene 8, below).*

The Library/Archive, the Cloister, and a stairwell up toward the bell tower all lead off from here,
unlocked. The heavy stone seal beneath the altar is the only thing not yet open.

---

## SCENE 4 — GUEST QUARTERS / INFIRMARY CELL

![Guest Quarters / Infirmary Cell — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_guest_quarters_concept.png)

Unlocked, off the Chapel. Torn bedsheets, twisted into makeshift restraints, still tied to the bed
frame — snapped rather than untied.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE ROOM]*

A cave map lies scattered across the floor, a tunnel junction circled in a hand Jim doesn't
recognize, a designation scrawled beside it: **VANGUARD ACCESS — JCT 4.**

> **JIM:** *"...I've seen this junction before."*

Movement from the far corner.

*Combat: a tougher single Shambler encounter — the injured Foundry worker sheltered here, fully
turned by the time Jim arrives.*

## SCENE 5 — THE SPRING / WELL CHAMBER (OPTIONAL)

![Spring / Well Chamber — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_spring_well_chamber_concept.png)

Open from the Chapel. The monastery's original natural spring, dark mineral sediment running
through water that should be clear.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE WATER]*

> **JIM:** *"...Yeah. That's not normal."*

No key items. Jim doesn't stay long.

## SCENE 6 — THE LIBRARY / ARCHIVE

![Library / Archive — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_library_archive_concept.png)

Unlocked. Cave maps, burial records, correspondence between earlier abbots.

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH ARCHIVE]*

> *"The Mercy That Should Not Be Asked For."*

Jim reads it once. Doesn't try to make it mean something yet.

A worn prayer book sits on a reading stand, a page marked with a ribbon — the traditional order of
the tower's daily bell calls.

*ITEM NOTED: PRAYER BOOK — Bell Tower clue 2 of 3.*

A separate note, tucked into the same book, mentions a hidden entrance somewhere on the ridge —
no key here, though. Just a lead.

---

## SCENE 7 — THE CLOISTER

![Cloister — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_cloister_concept.png)

Unlocked, off the Chapel. Sandbags, a barricaded colonnade, spent shell casings — the police's
defensive position during the standoff.

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH POSITION]*

A folded memorandum, Vanguard letterhead, left behind.

> *Internal correspondence acknowledging historical monastery records describing regenerative
> anomalies "consistent with current field observations," dated years before tonight.*

Jim folds it once and pockets it without saying anything.

Four weathered statues stand at the colonnade's four corners, each facing a different direction.

*ITEM NOTED: FOUR STATUES — Bell Tower clue 3 of 3.*

---

## SCENE 8 — THE BELL TOWER

![Bell Tower — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_bell_tower_concept.png)

Up the Chapel's side staircase: four bell ropes, a logbook, a view across the whole ridge and the
city burning quietly below it.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE LOGBOOK]*

An emergency ringing pattern, historically used for fire or disaster — not the pattern the mural,
the prayer book, and the cloister statues all point toward.

*Interaction prompt: [RING BELLS]*

Jim works out the order — Dawn, Labor, Mourning, Rest, the same sequence the mural showed, the
prayer book confirmed, the statues' own facing directions backed up a third time.

First bell.

> *(nothing)*

Second.

> *(a distant THUNK, somewhere below)*

Third.

> *(stone grinding beneath the chapel)*

Fourth.

The floor shifts under Jim's feet. Somewhere below, a circular stone seal retracts beneath the
altar.

> **JIM:** *"...There it is."*

The sound carries. Far below, on the ridge road, something that was moving away from the monastery
changes direction.

> **JIM:** *"...And there goes the element of surprise."*

---

## SCENE 9 — THE OLD SEAL / CRYPT DEPTHS

![The Old Seal / Crypt Depths — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_old_seal_crypt_depths_concept.png)

Down through the newly-opened seal: generations-old warning inscriptions, sealed burial niches.
Figures move ahead in the dark — calm, deliberate, faint mineral streaking visible along exposed
skin.

*Combat: [the Cave-Touched](../Creatures/The_Cave_Touched.md) (pack encounter) — infected changed
by direct proximity to Black Vein in the wider cave network, reached through Vanguard's illicit
tunnel connection, rather than a bite or a Vanguard facility.*

Once the last one goes down, Jim keeps moving rather than examining them too closely.

---

## SCENE 10 — BOSS ENCOUNTER: THE PENITENT

Deeper still, past the last burial niche, the passage widens into something that isn't quite a
room and isn't quite natural rock. A shape sits against the far wall, motionless, robes fused into
mineral-streaked growths that could pass for a burial effigy if Jim didn't already know better.

*Interaction prompt: [APPROACH]*

It doesn't move at first. Jim gets close enough to see a face — old, still recognizably a man's —
before it does.

> **JIM:** *"...Oh."*

*Combat: boss encounter — [The Penitent](../Creatures/The_Penitent.md). Slow, deliberate, weight
and inevitability rather than speed; doesn't pursue aggressively until Jim's already close.*

Once it's finally still, Jim doesn't say anything for a moment.

> **JIM:** *"...Somebody sealed you in here instead of killing you. That's not nothing."*

He doesn't know how long ago. Nothing here says.

---

## SCENE 11 — THE RELIQUARY

![The Reliquary — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_reliquary_concept.png)

Past The Penitent's chamber: a small, dignified sacred room, relics in glass cases, candles that
somehow still burn.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE CASE]*

A reliquary case holds a bronze, wedge-shaped medallion — the same wedge shape as the other four
emblems scattered across Ravenwood, whether or not Jim's found any of them yet.

*ITEM ACQUIRED: FAITH CREST*

A relief portrait: a hooded, bearded man. Cast beneath it:

> **ABBOT MATTHIAS KANE**
> **FOUNDER**
> **1887**

He looks around the small room once more.

**If this is Jim's fifth crest** (Faith completes the set):

> **JIM:** *"...Five."*

He doesn't say anything else. He knows exactly what that means now.

**If this is any other number** (the Monastery is open-order — Faith could be Jim's first crest
just as easily as his last):

He pockets it without a word.

> *Design note: an earlier version of this line always said "...Five," which only makes sense if
> Faith happens to be Jim's fifth crest. Since district order is fully open, this is a real
> contradiction if Faith is collected first, second, third, or fourth instead — branch on actual
> crest count at the moment of pickup, not the district's position in a fixed list.*

---

## SCENE 12 — THE SEALED PASSAGE / OLD CAVES

Past the Reliquary, a further descent — the oldest sealed section of all. No lock, no key: the same
mechanism Jim already solved to get this far left this passage open too.

*Interaction prompt: [ENTER PASSAGE]*

Ancient stone walls transition into raw cave rock. Deep-carved warning inscriptions line the way,
and a mineral vein glows faintly in the rock itself — the same dark branching pattern Jim's seen
before, at the Foundry, on a much smaller scale.

*Interaction prompt: [READ INSCRIPTIONS]*

> *"A vein without end."*

The oldest record here, barely legible, describes the first known discovery of Black Vein beneath
the ridge — centuries before Vanguard, before the town, before any of the names Jim's collected
tonight.

A small emergency recorder sits on a stone shelf, still running on some kind of backup power that
shouldn't still exist.

*Interaction prompt: [PLAY RECORDING]*

> **BROTHER CORMAC (RECORDING):** *"To whoever remains in Ravenwood."*
>
> (static)
>
> **BROTHER CORMAC (RECORDING):** *"The source is beneath us. It was here before Vanguard. Do not
> believe they discovered it."*
>
> (a long pause)
>
> **BROTHER CORMAC (RECORDING):** *"They opened what others had already learned to close."*

Static. The recording ends there.

Jim stands in the passage a long moment.

**If this is one of Jim's later crests (he's already seen Vanguard bury the truth elsewhere in
Ravenwood):**

> **JIM:** *"...Yeah. I got that part too."*

**If this is one of Jim's earlier crests (he doesn't have much to compare it to yet):**

> **JIM:** *"...Before Vanguard."*

He doesn't finish the thought out loud either way.

He turns back the way he came — Memorial Park, and whatever's actually underneath it, waiting.

> *Design note: this scene is self-contained within the Monastery — it doesn't require any other
> district to be completed first, and doesn't function as a guaranteed "last district" beat. If
> Jim reaches it early, it's an unplaceable mystery; if late, it reads as confirmation of a pattern
> he already suspected. See [`Locations/Monastery.md`](../Locations/Monastery.md) → "The Sealed
> Passage / Old Caves."*

## SCENE 13 — HILLSIDE RESIDENTIAL STREET (SECONDARY, OPTIONAL)

![Hillside Residential Street — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_hillside_residential_street_concept.png)

## SCENE 17 — THE HIDDEN ARCHIVE (OPTIONAL)

![Hidden Archive — room concept](../Assets/Reference/monastery_hidden_archive_concept.png)

Opened with the Archive Key, if Jim has it. Brother Cormac's own secret collection — original cave
