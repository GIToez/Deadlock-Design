# Chapter 2 — Worthy Academy

> Scene-by-scene script for the Southeast District — Knowledge Crest. Written 2026-08-14, the
> fourth of Chapter 2's five districts to receive this treatment, following
> [`Chapter_2_Police_Station.md`](Chapter_2_Police_Station.md),
> [`Chapter_2_Hospital.md`](Chapter_2_Hospital.md), and
> [`Chapter_2_Foundry.md`](Chapter_2_Foundry.md) — kept as its own standalone file per that
> convention, restarting its own Scene 1 count. Everything below scripts
> [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md) in full: dialogue, document text, interaction
> prompts, and the district's signature pack encounter. Per the locked convention in
> [`Assets/README.md`](../Assets/README.md), every scene below embeds the same room-concept image
> already generated for [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md), reused rather than
> regenerated. See [`STORY_NOTES.md`](../STORY_NOTES.md) for the Direction Log entry covering this
> pass, including the new [`Items/Key_Items/`](../Items/Key_Items/README.md) writeups it required.
>
> Treat names, exact dialogue, and specific combat staging as proposals pending review, same as any
> other new material. Principal Diane Ashford is never seen alive or dead in this script, per her
> own location file's "deliberately unresolved" convention — everything about her is reconstructed
> from her office and the final broadcast. Per that same file, no new creature type is introduced
> here — every encounter in this district is a standard [Shambler](../Creatures/Shambler.md), per
> the project owner's explicit framing that this location's horror is about people, not a monster.

## SCENE 1 — SOUTHEAST DISTRICT ENTRY / APPROACH

Jim leaves whichever part of Ravenwood he was in behind. The neighborhood softens before it
sharpens again — modest houses, a corner store, then a wide campus behind a low brick wall. Worthy
Academy comes into view, and for the first time tonight, something looks almost normal. School
banners still hang along the fence. Emergency lights glow steadily rather than flickering. A
basketball scoreboard, visible through a gymnasium window, sits frozen mid-game — **HOME 41,
VISITOR 38, 4TH QUARTER.** Student artwork lines the outer walls, laminated against the weather.

> **JIM:** *"...This one almost looks okay."*

Then he gets closer, and it doesn't.

> the deliberate contrast with the Foundry and Hospital's immediately-visible wrongness.*

![Front Entrance — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_front_entrance_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md)

The front entrance tells the story before Jim's inside it. Overturned barricades. A burned-out
vehicle in dark tactical colors, nose-first against a concrete planter. Spent shell casings, but
fewer than the Foundry's Loading Yard or the Hospital's Ambulance Bay — whatever happened here
didn't go on as long.

Taped to the doors themselves, at eye height, in the same handwriting Jim's starting to recognize
from a dozen scrawled notes tonight: a single sheet of paper.

*Interaction prompt: [READ SIGN]*

> *"You heard him."*

No further explanation. Jim looks at it a moment.

> **JIM:** *"Somebody made a choice and wanted it on the record."*

*Interaction prompt: [OPEN DOORS]*

The doors give easily — nothing's actually holding them shut anymore, just the memory of a
barricade that did its job once.

## SCENE 3 — THE GYMNASIUM (HUB)

![Gymnasium — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_gymnasium_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md)
for the full room writeup.*

Inside, the contrast breaks completely. Hundreds of cots stretch across the gym floor, most empty
now, blankets kicked aside or still neatly folded, waiting for people who never came back for them.
Food trays sit half-eaten. A children's play area in one corner still has toys arranged the way a
tired volunteer left them, not the way a child would. Police barricades — office furniture,
folding tables — line the exits from the inside.

A large whiteboard dominates the near wall, five columns ruled across it: **INSIDE / HOSPITAL /
POLICE / MISSING / UNKNOWN.**

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE BOARD]*

Names fill every column, some crossed out and rewritten in a different column entirely, some just
left under **MISSING** with nothing else added after. Jim reads a few names. None of them mean
anything to him yet.

> **JIM:** *"...This was supposed to be the safe one."*

Jim can see the shape of the district immediately, same as everywhere else tonight. Four sealed
points from here:

- **ADMINISTRATION OFFICE** — keyed lock.
- **AUDITORIUM** — keyed lock.
- **LIBRARY** — barricaded from the inside.
- **MAINTENANCE BASEMENT** — keyed lock.

One doorway stands open with no lock at all: the **CAFETERIA.**

A shambler in a torn volunteer vest — a lanyard still around its neck reading **VOLUNTEER STAFF** —
shuffles between two rows of empty cots.

*Combat: Shambler.*

Once it's down, the gym goes quiet except for a distant PA speaker crackling somewhere overhead,
too faint to make out clearly yet.

> *Design note: scripts* [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md) *→ "Storyline," "The
> Gymnasium (hub)" — the INSIDE/HOSPITAL/POLICE/MISSING/UNKNOWN board and the four visibly locked
> points, same "see the shape of what's left to solve" convention as every other main location.*

## SCENE 5 — THE ADMINISTRATION OFFICE

![Administration Office — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_administration_office_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md)
for the full room writeup.*

The Administration Office Key opens a small suite of desks and filing cabinets — the ordinary
paperwork of running a school, still visible under everything that's happened since. A clipboard of
shelter registration rosters sits on the front desk, names and information filled in by hand as
people arrived.

*Interaction prompt: [READ RECORDS]*

Names, addresses, medical conditions, employment, hospitalization history — hundreds of entries.
Clipped to the top page, a printed request on unfamiliar letterhead:

> **VANGUARD PUBLIC SAFETY COORDINATION — REQUEST FOR COMPLETE SHELTER ROSTER**
>
> *Please provide full registration information for all sheltered individuals, including
> employment history, for evacuation-planning purposes. Priority flag: any individual with current
> or prior Steelgate Refinery employment.*

Jim reads the last line twice.

> **JIM:** *"Evacuation planning. Sure."*

### Optional — a handwritten note, folded into the roster clipboard

*Interaction prompt: [READ NOTE]*

> *"I gave them the Harris family."*
>
> *"All three."*

No context, no signature — just those two lines, written by someone who clearly needed to write
them down somewhere.

> **JIM:** *"...Harris."*

The name means something, though Jim can't place exactly what yet — a torn slip he found somewhere
else tonight, maybe.

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH DESK]*

A second key ring sits in the top drawer, tagged **"ISOLATION."**

*ITEM ACQUIRED: ISOLATION WING KEY*

> *Design/story note: scripts* [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md) *→ "Outbreak

The Isolation Wing Key opens what used to be the nurse's office and two adjacent classrooms,

> Fever · Confusion · Unexplained bleeding · Violent behavior · Severe muscle spasms · Unusual

## SCENE 12 — THE EAST ACADEMIC WING / SCIENCE ROOMS

![East Academic Wing / Science Rooms — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_east_wing_science_rooms_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md)
for the full room writeup.*

A fire door blocks the east academic wing — not locked, jammed, warped slightly in its frame from
whatever forced it shut in the first place.

*Interaction prompt: [USE FIRE AXE]*

The axe splits the frame rather than the door itself; it swings open with a shriek of bent metal.
Beyond it: a stretch of science classrooms, lab tables overturned, chemical-safety posters still
taped to the walls above the chaos. Several shapes move between the tables — more than one, closer
together than any single encounter tonight.

> **JIM:** *"...Okay. Here we go."*

*Combat: multiple Shamblers — turned Isolation Wing patients and the civilians trapped alongside
them when the fire doors sealed. Standard Shamblers throughout; the Academy deliberately introduces
no new creature type, per* [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md)*'s explicit framing
that this district's horror is about people, not a monster. The district's signature encounter,
fought as a group in a cramped, table-cluttered space that limits maneuvering room.*

Once the last one goes down, Jim checks the nearest body out of habit rather than expectation. A key
ring is clipped to a lanyard around its neck, still reading **PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE — STAFF ONLY.**

*Interaction prompt: [TAKE PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE KEY]*

*ITEM ACQUIRED: PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE KEY*

> **JIM:** *"...Staff. Not a student."*

He doesn't say anything else about it.

## SCENE 14 — FOUNDER'S HALL

![Founder's Hall — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_founders_hall_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md)
for the full room writeup.*

The Founder's Hall Key opens a small trophy and alumni hall, tucked away from the rest of the
school's daily traffic — display cases of school history, decades of team photographs, a framed
document under glass near the entrance.

*Interaction prompt: [READ FOUNDING DOCUMENT]*

> *Worthy Academy was chartered in 1887 by five incorporators of the Town of Ravenwood, each
> pledging support to a distinct civic institution: Order, Medicine, Industry, Faith, and
> Knowledge. In commemoration, five ceremonial medallions were struck — one per founder, one per

Jim reads it twice.

> **JIM:** *"...There it is. In writing."*

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE CASE]*

A glass display case near the back holds a bronze, wedge-shaped medallion, the same shape as the
three Jim already carries. A relief portrait fills most of it — a composed woman in formal
period dress, a stack of books carved beside her shoulder. Cast into the metal beneath her:

> **ELEANOR WORTHY**
> **FOUNDER**
> **1887**

Below her name, a small relief of a torch.

> **JIM:** *"Eleanor Worthy. Founder."*

*Interaction prompt: [OPEN CASE]*

*ITEM ACQUIRED: KNOWLEDGE CREST*

He looks at the case a moment longer, then at the rest of the quiet little hall around him.

> **JIM:** *"Four down."*

## SCENE 15 — THE MAINTENANCE ESCAPE CORRIDOR

Past Founder's Hall, a plain service corridor leads toward a rear exit — utility lighting, exposed
pipe, nothing built for anyone but staff to ever see.

*Interaction prompt: [ENTER CORRIDOR]*

*ITEM ACQUIRED (mandatory, story beat): —*

Jim walks the corridor slowly. Small signs of a hurried, careful evacuation line the way — a child's
single shoe, a folded blanket someone set down and never came back for, scuff marks suggesting
several people moving quickly but not chaotically.

Near the end, close to the rear door itself: a police badge, set down deliberately rather than
dropped, a folded note tucked beneath it.

*Interaction prompt: [READ NOTE]*

> *"If they make it out, that's enough."*

No name. Jim picks up the badge, turns it over once, and sets it back down exactly where it was.

> **JIM:** *"...Yeah. It is."*

He pushes through the rear door into the rain and doesn't look back at the corridor again.

> *Design/story note: scripts* [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md) *→ "Storyline,"
> "The Maintenance Escape Corridor" — a mandatory, non-combat story beat reached only after the
> Knowledge Crest is already in hand, per that file's explicit design intent (the location's true
> emotional climax, placed after the gameplay reward rather than gating it, same convention as the
> Foundry's Black Vein Cavern). Whether the escaping group survives is deliberately left
> unresolved, per [`Locations/Academy.md`](../Locations/Academy.md) → "Unresolved Ideas."*
