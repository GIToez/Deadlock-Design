## SCENE 1 — SOUTHEAST DISTRICT ENTRY / APPROACH

Jim leaves whichever part of Ravenwood he was in behind. The neighborhood softens before it
sharpens again — modest houses, a corner store, then a wide campus behind a low brick wall. Worthy
Academy comes into view, and for the first time tonight, something looks almost normal. School
banners still hang along the fence. Emergency lights glow steadily rather than flickering. A
basketball scoreboard, visible through a gymnasium window, sits frozen mid-game — **HOME 41,
VISITOR 38, 4TH QUARTER.**

> **JIM:** *"...This one almost looks okay."*

Then he gets closer, and it doesn't.

## SCENE 2 — FRONT ENTRANCE / BARRICADES

![Front Entrance — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_front_entrance_concept.png)

The front entrance tells the story before Jim's inside it. Overturned barricades. A burned-out
vehicle in dark tactical colors. Spent shell casings, fewer than the Foundry's Loading Yard or the
Hospital's Ambulance Bay.

Taped to the doors at eye height: a single sheet of paper.

*Interaction prompt: [READ SIGN]*

> *"You heard him."*

No further explanation. Jim looks at it a moment, then keeps moving.

*Interaction prompt: [OPEN DOORS]*

The doors give easily.

## SCENE 3 — THE GYMNASIUM

![Gymnasium — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_gymnasium_concept.png)

Inside, the contrast breaks completely. Hundreds of cots stretch across the gym floor, most empty
now. Food trays sit half-eaten. A children's play area in one corner still has toys arranged the
way a tired volunteer left them, not the way a child would. Police barricades line the exits from
the inside.

A large whiteboard dominates the near wall: **INSIDE / HOSPITAL / POLICE / MISSING / UNKNOWN.**

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE BOARD]*

Names fill every column, some crossed out and rewritten entirely, some just left under **MISSING**
with nothing else added after.

> **JIM:** *"...This was supposed to be the safe one."*

Jim doesn't stop to size the building up. The Cafeteria's open archway pulls him forward first —
the Administration Office, the Auditorium, the Library, and the Maintenance Basement only start to
matter once he's already deeper into the campus.

A shambler in a torn volunteer vest — a lanyard reading **VOLUNTEER STAFF** — shuffles between two
rows of empty cots.

*Combat: Shambler (staff/volunteer clothing).*

### Optional — the trophy case

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE TROPHY CASE]*

A wall case near the entrance, mostly athletic trophies — but one shelf holds a small engraved
plaque commemorating the school's own founding, half a century of student council presidents'
names beneath it.

*ITEM NOTED: FOUNDING PLAQUE — one of eight historical clue objects toward the Archive Puzzle (see
Scene 9, below); Jim doesn't need to understand its significance yet.*

## SCENE 9 — THE LIBRARY: THE ARCHIVE PUZZLE

![Library — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_library_concept.png)

Unlocked, off the Gymnasium. Beyond the entrance: picture books, a reading corner rearranged into
something closer to a nest than a classroom activity.

*Interaction prompt: [READ NOTEBOOK]*

A teacher's notebook sits on the reading-corner rug.

> *"Told them it's a sleepover. Some of them believe it." / "Do not tell the younger ones the
> police are gone." / "They keep asking when their parents are coming." / "I don't know what to
> tell them anymore."*

Jim sets the notebook down carefully.

> **JIM:** *"...Yeah."*

A large historical timeline dominates the back wall: **FOUNDING OF RAVENWOOD → STEELGATE OPENS →
ST. DYMPHNA FOUNDED → RPD ESTABLISHED → WORTHY ACADEMY OPENS → MONASTERY BUILT.** Several of its
plaques sit removed or shifted, leaning against the shelf beneath it. A retired teacher's own
research notes, tucked into a nearby returns bin, and a hallway plaque salvaged from outside the
gym round out what's available here.

*ITEM NOTED: RETIRED TEACHER'S NOTES / HALLWAY PLAQUE — two more of eight historical clue objects,
found directly in this room.*

*Interaction prompt: [RECONSTRUCT TIMELINE]*

Between whatever Jim's already found elsewhere on campus (any four or five of the eight objects
are enough — a founder portrait, an old newspaper, a yearbook, the trophy case's history booklet,
this room's own teacher's notes and hallway plaque) and the timeline's own five loose plaques, the
chronology becomes readable: **Authority, Faith, Industry, Medicine, Knowledge** — in that order,
per what Jim's actually gathered rather than a fixed answer key.

One detail doesn't sit quite right: an official record dates Steelgate's opening to 1968, but the
old yearbook, if Jim found it, mentions mining activity years before that. Nothing here explains
the discrepancy.

*Interaction prompt: [PLACE PLAQUES]*

Jim sets the five plaques into the timeline in order.

> **CLICK.**

A mechanical catch releases behind the display — no electronics, nothing but an old physical
latch finally letting go. The whole timeline swings outward on a hidden hinge.

## SCENE 10 — PRINCIPAL ASHFORD'S HIDDEN ARCHIVE

Behind the timeline: a cramped space, deliberately hidden, holding exactly what someone needed to
protect in a hurry — a few boxes of records, and, on a small stand, a bronze, wedge-shaped
medallion.

*Interaction prompt: [TAKE MEDALLION]*

*ITEM ACQUIRED: KNOWLEDGE CREST*

A relief portrait fills most of it — a composed woman in formal period dress, a stack of books
carved beside her shoulder.

> **ELEANOR WORTHY**
> **FOUNDER**
> **1887**

He looks back at the swung-open timeline.

> **JIM:** *"Four down."*

## SCENE 13 — THE EAST ACADEMIC WING / SCIENCE ROOMS

![East Academic Wing / Science Rooms — room concept](../Assets/Reference/academy_east_wing_science_rooms_concept.png)

A fire door blocks the east academic wing — jammed, not locked.

*Interaction prompt: [USE FIRE AXE]*

The axe splits the frame; it swings open with a shriek of bent metal. Beyond it: a stretch of
science classrooms, lab tables overturned. Several shapes move between the tables.

> **JIM:** *"...Okay. Here we go."*

*Combat: multiple Shamblers (staff/parent/student clothing) — turned Isolation Wing patients and
civilians trapped when the fire doors sealed.*

Once the last one goes down, a heavier door at the far end of the science wing, previously ignored
as a supply closet, is standing open — something inside moved recently.

*Interaction prompt: [ENTER BACK CLASSROOM]*

A woman is seated at a small desk, surrounded by a handful of cots, arms thickened and root-like
along the forearms, still recognizably a person from the neck up. She doesn't look up right away.

*Combat: boss encounter — [The Matron](../Creatures/The_Matron.md). She doesn't pursue
aggressively at first; her aggression escalates the longer Jim stays near the room behind her.*

Once she's down, Jim doesn't linger over her the way he has with some of tonight's other losses.

> **JIM:** *"...I don't know what you thought you were still protecting."*

A key ring is clipped to her belt, tagged **PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE — STAFF ONLY.**

*ITEM ACQUIRED: PRINCIPAL'S OFFICE KEY*

## SCENE 16 — THE MAINTENANCE ESCAPE CORRIDOR

Past Founder's Hall, a plain service corridor leads toward a rear exit.

*Interaction prompt: [ENTER CORRIDOR]*

Jim walks the corridor slowly. A child's single shoe. A folded blanket someone set down and never
came back for.

Near the end, close to the rear door: a police badge, set down deliberately, a folded note tucked
beneath it.

*Interaction prompt: [READ NOTE]*

> *"If they make it out, that's enough."*

No name.

> **JIM:** *"...Yeah. It is."*

He pushes through the rear door into the rain.
