## SCENE 1 — NORTHWEST DISTRICT ENTRY / APPROACH

Jim leaves whichever part of Ravenwood he was in behind. The skyline changes first — smokestacks,
most dark now, one still faintly glowing from residual heat against the storm clouds. Warning
lights rotate through smoke-hazed windows in a slow, useless rhythm. Somewhere inside, machinery is
still audibly running on old backup power, a low mechanical groan under everything else.

A looping automated announcement echoes across the yard, tinny through an outdoor speaker system.

> **PA (RECORDED, LOOPING):** *"Attention. Evacuate via nearest marked exit. This is not a
> drill."*

It cycles again as Jim gets closer. And again. Nobody's coming.

> **JIM:** *"Yeah. I got that the first time."*

He keeps walking toward the gate.

## SCENE 2 — THE LOADING YARD

![Loading Yard — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_loading_yard_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](../Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md) for the full room writeup.*

Through the outer gate: the Loading Yard, and the aftermath of a fight that didn't end cleanly for
anyone. Tools and personal effects lie scattered where they were dropped mid-flight — a hard hat, a
lunch cooler, a torn work glove. A chain-link gate stands forced open, bent off one hinge. Spent
shell casings are scattered across the wet concrete. At least one body lies in Vanguard-marked
tactical gear; several more, a short distance away, in Foundry work uniforms.

> **JIM:** *"...Nobody won this one."*

Idle forklifts sit abandoned at odd angles, blocking the direct path and forcing Jim to pick his way
around them toward the plant's main entrance.

### Optional — the bodies

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE VANGUARD OPERATOR]*

Nothing on the body identifies him beyond the tactical gear itself — no name, no rank insignia, just
a plain black patch reading **VANGUARD — SITE SECURITY.**

> **JIM:** *"Company man. Didn't even get a name on the uniform."*

Signs throughout the yard point deeper into the plant and downward at the same time — **LOWER
PROCESSING**, **AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY**, **GEOLOGICAL ACCESS**, **VANGUARD RESTRICTED AREA.**
Jim reads them on his way past.

> **JIM:** *"Geological access. In a metal foundry."*

## SCENE 3 — THE CASTING HALL (HUB)

![Casting Hall — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_casting_hall_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](../Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md) for the full room writeup.*

Through the main entrance: a vast industrial floor, heat radiating off at least one still-molten
holding vat, its orange glow throwing long shadows across idle conveyor lines. An overhead crane
hangs motionless mid-track. The air smells like hot metal and something else underneath it Jim
doesn't try to identify.

Jim can see the shape of the district immediately, same as the station and the hospital before it.
Three sealed points from here:

- **PLANT MANAGER'S OFFICE** — keyed lock.
- **VANGUARD SITE OFFICE** — a separate, sleek corporate-style lock, out of place against the rest
  of the hall's industrial hardware.
- **RESTRICTED ELEVATOR** — a card-reader lock, dark and unresponsive.

Two doorways stand open with no lock at all: one toward the **FOUNDRY CLINIC**, one toward the
**BREAK ROOM.**

> **JIM:** *"...Same shape again. Different building."*

Movement near one of the conveyor lines — a shambler in a torn Foundry uniform, moving slow and
wrong between the idle machinery.

*Combat: Shambler.*

Once it's down, the hall goes quiet except for the low groan of the still-running backup power and
the faint crackle of the holding vat's residual heat.

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](../Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md) for the full room writeup.*

Off the Casting Hall: a small on-site first-aid room, barely bigger than a closet. A restraint
gurney sits in the middle of the room, straps snapped rather than unbuckled. A wall phone hangs off
its cradle, still connected, a faint dial tone audible when Jim gets close.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE PHONE / CALL LOG]*

A clipboard hangs beside the phone, a nurse's handwriting recording her own calls in order.

> *"11:47 PM — Called Ravenwood Memorial. Described symptoms. Advised to keep monitoring."*
>
> *"12:03 AM — Called Vanguard emergency line per protocol. Reported worsening condition,
> unexplained rapid healing."*
>
> *"12:06 AM — Vanguard response: 'Do not administer further treatment.'"*
>
> *"12:07 AM — Asked why. No answer given."*

The log ends there. Jim sets the clipboard back down slowly.

> **JIM:** *"She figured it out faster than most people got the chance to."*

Movement from the gurney — what's restrained there is long past needing restraints. It tears free
the rest of the way as Jim turns.

*Combat: a tougher single Shambler encounter — Outbreak Night beat 11's worker, fully turned by the
time Jim arrives.*

Once it's down, Jim finds a key ring hanging on a hook by the door, a paper tag reading **"MGR
OFFICE"** in the same handwriting as the call log.

*Interaction prompt: [TAKE MANAGER'S OFFICE KEY]*

*ITEM ACQUIRED: MANAGER'S OFFICE KEY*

> **JIM:** *"Left in a hurry. Can't blame her."*

soot-streaked window. A nameplate on the desk reads **D. FITCH — PLANT MANAGER.** A landline sits

never came back to hang it up properly.

*Interaction prompt: [READ DESK NOTES]*

A yellow legal pad on the desk, filled with a manager's shorthand — clearly notes taken during a
phone call, transcribed in real time.

> *"Asked what happened underground. No answer."*
>
> *"Asked why we aren't evacuating."*
>
> *"Response: 'Evacuation poses an unacceptable contamination risk.'"*
>
> *"Asked: contamination risk to who?"*

> **JIM:** *"He asked the right question. Just to the wrong people."*

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE INTERCOM]*

A plant-wide intercom control sits on the corner of the desk, one button still glowing faintly amber

> **JIM:** *"...This is what he used."*

### Optional — desk drawer

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH DRAWER]*

A plant-issue access card sits alone in an otherwise empty drawer, placed there deliberately rather
than dropped.

## SCENE 13 — THE SECURITY CHECKPOINT

![Security Checkpoint — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_security_checkpoint_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](../Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md) for the full room writeup.*

A reinforced door blocks the corridor toward Lower Processing — thick, riveted, built to hold
against something, not just to lock a room.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE DOOR]*

> **JIM:** *"No card reader. No regular lock. Somebody wanted this shut for good."*

*Interaction prompt: [USE CUTTING TORCH]*

Sparks scatter across the concrete as Jim works the torch along the seam. It takes longer than the
padlocks and deadbolts he's dealt with tonight — this door was never meant to be forced. Eventually
the metal gives, and the door swings inward on its own weight.

Beyond it: several figures, still upright, moving with a steadiness none of tonight's shamblers have
had. Foundry work coveralls, fused rather than torn where mutated tissue has grown through the
fabric. Old, healed-over discoloration rather than fresh wounds — the physical evidence of years,
not one night.

> **JIM:** *"...These aren't fresh."*

*Combat: [the Exposure Cohort](../Creatures/Exposure_Cohort.md) — several long-term-exposed
Foundry workers, encountered together as a pack rather than isolated individuals. More coordinated
and dangerous than an equivalent number of Shamblers — steadier, more deliberate in how they close
distance, reflecting years of slow adaptation rather than a single night's chaotic transformation.
The district's signature encounter.*

Once the last one goes down, Jim stands there a moment longer than the fight requires.

> **JIM:** *"...Twelve years, some of you. Maybe more."*

A key falls loose from one of them as it collapses — heavier, older than anything else Jim's found
in this building.

*Interaction prompt: [TAKE BOARDROOM KEY]*

*ITEM ACQUIRED: BOARDROOM KEY*

## SCENE 16 — THE BLACK VEIN CAVERN

![The Black Vein Cavern — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_black_vein_cavern_concept.png)

*AI-generated room concept, for visual reference — see [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](../Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md) for the full room writeup.*

Past the Boardroom, a short passage where all human construction gradually stops. Reinforced
concrete gives way to older mine tunnel, then bare rock, then a threshold that isn't built at all —
a natural cave mouth, cold air moving through it from somewhere much deeper than the plant ever
reached.

*Interaction prompt: [ENTER CAVERN]*

Jim steps through. And stops.

Black Vein isn't contained in any lab, any sample jar, any vial with a case number. It runs through
the cavern walls themselves — massive, branching, ancient-looking formations, dark and glistening
faintly in whatever light reaches this deep. It doesn't move. It doesn't need to.

> **JIM:** *"...Jesus."*

He doesn't approach it. He doesn't need to.

> **JIM:** *"This is what all of it's been about. The whole night."*

A small recorder sits on a flat stone near the threshold, left there deliberately rather than
dropped.

*Interaction prompt: [PLAY RECORDER]*

> **VOICE (RECORDING, FITCH OR A SURVIVING FOREMAN):** *"Anyone receiving this — stay away from the
> Foundry. Whatever Vanguard tells you, don't come down here."*

A long pause. Just the sound of the cavern's cold air moving in the background.

> **VOICE (RECORDING):** *"They didn't find this thing tonight. They've been digging at it for
> years."*

Static. The recording ends there.

Jim stands at the threshold a while longer, looking at the vein running through the rock, before he
finally turns back the way he came.

> **JIM:** *"...Yeah. I got that part."*

> *Design/story note: scripts* [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](../Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md)
> *→ "Storyline," "The Black Vein Cavern," and "Outbreak Night," beat 22 — a mandatory, non-combat
