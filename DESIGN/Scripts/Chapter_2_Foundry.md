# Chapter 2 — Steelgate Refinery

> Scene-by-scene script for the Northwest District — Industry Crest. Rewritten (2026-08-14) to
> match [`Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md`](../Locations/Foundry_Refinery.md)'s **Casting Puzzle** —
> see [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) → "Five Puzzle Philosophies." The previous version (a Clinic →
> Manager's Office → Restricted Elevator key chain) is fully superseded; full rationale in
> [`STORY_NOTES.md`](../STORY_NOTES.md). This rewrite also fixes an internal contradiction the
> Industry Crest was located in *two* places at once (the seized mold, per the puzzle redesign, and
> the Founder's Boardroom's display case, per the older text) — the Boardroom's case is now empty,
> and the crest comes only from the Casting Line.
>
> Plant Manager Daniel Fitch is never seen alive or dead in this script, per his own file's
> "deliberately unresolved" convention.

---

## SCENE 1 — NORTHWEST DISTRICT ENTRY / APPROACH

Jim leaves whichever part of Ravenwood he was in behind. The skyline changes first — smokestacks,
most dark now, one still faintly glowing from residual heat against the storm clouds. Machinery is
still audibly running on old backup power, a low mechanical groan under everything else.

> **PA (RECORDED, LOOPING):** *"Attention. Evacuate via nearest marked exit. This is not a
> drill."*

It cycles again as Jim gets closer. Nobody's coming.

### Crest-count branch (per [`Characters/Jim_Mercer.md`](../Characters/Jim_Mercer.md))

**0–1 crests (Survival / Suspicion):**

> **JIM:** *"Yeah. I got that the first time."*

**2–3 crests (Pattern / Anger):**

> **JIM:** *"Nobody's coming to any of these."*

**4+ crests (Resolve / Acceptance):**

Jim doesn't react to the recording at all. It's just noise now.

## SCENE 2 — THE LOADING YARD

![Loading Yard — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_loading_yard_concept.png)

Through the outer gate: the aftermath of a fight that didn't end cleanly for anyone. Tools and
personal effects lie scattered where they were dropped mid-flight. A chain-link gate stands forced
open, bent off one hinge. At least one body lies in Vanguard-marked tactical gear; several more,
a short distance away, in Foundry work uniforms.

> **JIM:** *"...Nobody won this one."*

Idle forklifts sit abandoned at odd angles, forcing Jim to pick his way around them.

### Optional — the bodies

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE VANGUARD OPERATOR]*

Nothing on the body identifies him beyond the tactical gear itself — a plain black patch reading
**VANGUARD — SITE SECURITY.**

Signs throughout the yard point deeper into the plant and downward at the same time — **LOWER
PROCESSING**, **AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY**, **GEOLOGICAL ACCESS**, **VANGUARD RESTRICTED AREA.**

> **JIM:** *"Geological access. In a metal foundry."*

## SCENE 3 — THE CASTING HALL

![Casting Hall — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_casting_hall_concept.png)

Through the main entrance, the seized casting line dominates the room before Jim understands
anything else about it — cold conveyor lines, an overhead crane frozen dead mid-track, and at the
center, a heavy mold carriage locked solid, still radiating leftover heat from whatever last
happened here. Warning gauges around the furnace sit dark. It's obviously the point of this room.

> **JIM:** *"...That's not going anywhere without help."*

The Plant Manager's Office, the Vanguard Site Office, and the Restricted Elevator all lead off
from here, unlocked doorways toward the Foundry Clinic and the Break Room besides.

Movement near one of the conveyor lines — a shambler in a torn Foundry uniform.

*Combat: Shambler (work coveralls).*

Once it's down, the hall goes quiet except for the low groan of backup power.

## SCENE 4 — THE FOUNDRY CLINIC

![Foundry Clinic — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_clinic_concept.png)

Off the Casting Hall: a small on-site first-aid room. A restraint gurney sits in the middle,
straps snapped rather than unbuckled. A wall phone hangs off its cradle, still connected.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE PHONE / CALL LOG]*

A clipboard hangs beside the phone, a nurse's handwriting recording her own calls in order.

> *"11:47 PM — Called Ravenwood Memorial. Described symptoms. Advised to keep monitoring."*
>
> *"12:03 AM — Called Vanguard emergency line per protocol. Reported worsening condition,
> unexplained rapid healing."*
>
> *"12:06 AM — Vanguard response: 'Do not administer further treatment.'"*
>
> *"12:07 AM — Asked why. No answer given."*

The log ends there. Jim sets the clipboard back down slowly.

The wall phone itself is still connected — not dead, just unanswered. Jim picks it up out of habit
more than hope. A crossed line bleeds through, someone else's call on a shared trunk line, too
faint and too brief to place.

> **VOICE (PHONE, DISTANT):** *"—can't hold the west corridor much longer, we need—"*

The line goes dead before whoever it was finishes the sentence. Jim doesn't know who that was, or
where, or whether "the west corridor" means anything he'll ever find out. He hangs up.

Movement from the gurney — what's restrained there is long past needing restraints.

*Combat: a tougher single Shambler encounter — Outbreak Night beat 11's worker, fully turned.*

## SCENE 6 — THE PLANT MANAGER'S OFFICE

![Plant Manager's Office — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_managers_office_concept.png)

Unlocked, off the Casting Hall — a modest office overlooking the floor through a soot-streaked
window. A nameplate reads **D. FITCH — PLANT MANAGER.**

*Interaction prompt: [READ DESK NOTES]*

A yellow legal pad, filled with a manager's shorthand from a phone call.

> *"Asked what happened underground. No answer." / "Asked why we aren't evacuating." / "Response:
> 'Evacuation poses an unacceptable contamination risk.'" / "Asked: contamination risk to who?" /
> "No answer."*

Jim sets the pad down without saying anything.

Clipped to a maintenance clipboard beside the notes, easy to overlook: a furnace inspection log,
listing a specific safe operating pressure range.

*ITEM ACQUIRED: FURNACE PRESSURE LOG*

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE INTERCOM]*

A plant-wide intercom control sits on the desk, one button glowing faintly amber — **ALL-CALL**,
held open longer than a normal announcement would need.

> **JIM:** *"...This is what he used."*

### Optional — desk drawer

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH DRAWER]*

A plant-issue access card, placed there deliberately rather than dropped.

*ITEM ACQUIRED: RESTRICTED ELEVATOR ACCESS CARD*

## SCENE 7 — THE VANGUARD SITE OFFICE

![Vanguard Site Office — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_vanguard_site_office_concept.png)

Unlocked, off the Casting Hall — a jarringly clean, out-of-place corporate office dropped into the
middle of an industrial plant.

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH DESK]*

A locked-out manual override for the Casting Hall's overhead crane sits mounted on the wall,
clearly disabled for ordinary plant use.

*Interaction prompt: [ENABLE OVERRIDE]*

*ITEM ACQUIRED: CRANE OVERRIDE*

> **JIM:** *"Corporate office with its own crane control. Sure."*

### Document — research contracts and funding records

> earliest dated document:
>
> *"VANGUARD INDUSTRIES — SITE ACQUISITION MEMORANDUM — STEELGATE REFINERY — 1968."*

> **JIM:** *"...1968."*

### Optional — the locked filing cabinet

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE CABINET]*

Separate from anything the crane needs — the Vanguard Site Key, from the Loading Docks, opens it.
Bonus paperwork only.

## SCENE 8 — OPERATING THE CASTING LINE

Back in the Casting Hall, Jim has what he needs to try the line.

*Interaction prompt: [RESTORE FURNACE FUEL FLOW]*

Working from the pressure log, Jim opens the gas valves by hand, watching the needle climb toward
the range Fitch's own notes describe. Too far and a relief valve screams open on its own; he backs
off, tries again, gets it to hold.

> **JIM:** *"...There."*

*Interaction prompt: [OPERATE CRANE]*

The override wakes the overhead crane. Jim works it by hand — left, right, forward, its shadow
sweeping visibly across the floor below — until the casting ladle sits directly over the seized
mold's own receptacle.

*Interaction prompt: [HEAT MOLD]*

The furnace's heat expands the collar holding the mold shut, just enough. It doesn't move yet.

> **JIM:** *"Almost. Needs to cool fast, not just heat up."*

The cooling line's own gauge sits dead. Whatever feeds it, it isn't running.

## SCENE 9 — THE RESTRICTED ELEVATOR

![Restricted Elevator — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_restricted_elevator_concept.png)

The access card wakes a card-reader beside a heavy freight elevator door.

*Interaction prompt: [USE ACCESS CARD]*

The elevator descends slowly, and the architectural transition is immediate: industrial brick and
exposed steel give way to sterile reinforced concrete.

> **JIM:** *"...Somebody built this on purpose."*

A manual valve beside the shaft, tagged and chained shut, blocks a pipe run marked **CASTING
LINE — COOLANT SUPPLY.**

*Interaction prompt: [CUT CHAIN / OPEN VALVE]*

> **JIM:** *"They didn't want this running again either."*

*ITEM STATE CHANGED: COOLING LINE VALVE — OPEN*

## SCENE 10 — THE CASTING LINE (COMPLETION)

Back at the Casting Hall, the cooling gauge has come alive.

*Interaction prompt: [ENGAGE COOLING LINE]*

Water blasts across the casting bed. Thermal contraction snaps the collar with a sound loud enough
that Jim braces before he understands what it was. The ancient mold separates.

> **CLANG.**

Inside: a bronze, wedge-shaped medallion.

*ITEM ACQUIRED: INDUSTRY CREST*

> **JIM:** *"...There you are."*

Beneath the machinery, where the mold carriage sat, a second thing is now visible — a shaft going
down, older and rougher than anything Vanguard built.

> **JIM:** *"...Of course."*

He doesn't finish the thought out loud.

## SCENE 13 — THE EXPOSURE RECORDS ROOM

![Exposure Records Room — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_exposure_records_concept.png)

Unlocked, off the old shaft's own corridor below the Casting Hall: a records room, employee files
organized by handwritten dividers reading **EXPOSURE COHORT A**, **COHORT B**, **COHORT C.**

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH FILES]*

Jim pulls one at random. Years of bloodwork, neurological testing, bone-density scans — the same
"normal for the job" complaints from the Break Room note, now filed and quantified.

> **JIM:** *"...This is the same guy from the locker room note."*

He checks the name against Scene 5. It matches.

> **JIM:** *"Twelve years of this."*

### Optional — a torn evacuation slip

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH FILES FURTHER]*

A single torn evacuation-registry slip, listing a Foundry worker, his wife, and their teenage son —
the Harris family.

> **JIM:** *"...Harris. Why do I—"*

He doesn't finish the thought. He pockets the slip anyway.

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH DRAWER]*

A door key tagged **"RESEARCH BAY."**

*ITEM ACQUIRED: RESEARCH BAY KEY*

## SCENE 15 — THE SECURITY CHECKPOINT

![Security Checkpoint — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_security_checkpoint_concept.png)

A reinforced door blocks the corridor toward Lower Processing.

*Interaction prompt: [USE CUTTING TORCH]*

Sparks scatter across the concrete. Eventually the metal gives, and the door swings inward.

Beyond it: several figures, still upright, moving with a steadiness none of tonight's shamblers
have had. Foundry work coveralls, fused rather than torn where mutated tissue has grown through
the fabric. At the center of them, larger than the rest, a segment of conveyor guard rail grown
into one forearm.

> **JIM:** *"...These aren't fresh."*

*Combat: boss encounter — The Foreman (boss-tier) and two or three standard Exposure Cohort
members (see [`Creatures/Exposure_Cohort.md`](../Creatures/Exposure_Cohort.md) → "The Foreman").
The pack fights together; The Foreman is the real damage threat.*

Once the last one goes down, Jim stands there a moment longer than the fight requires.

> **JIM:** *"...Twelve years, some of you. Maybe more."*

A key falls loose from The Foreman as he collapses.

*ITEM ACQUIRED: BOARDROOM KEY*

## SCENE 17 — THE FOUNDER'S BOARDROOM

![Founder's Boardroom — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_boardroom_concept.png)

The Boardroom Key opens a small, incongruously formal room this deep underground — a long polished
table, a row of framed portraits, and, at the far end, a glass display case, lit from within,
empty. Its felt backing still shows a wedge-shaped outline of what used to sit there.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE CASE]*

> **JIM:** *"...Already got that one."*

A small founding plaque beside the case:

> **ELIAS THORNE**
> **FOUNDER**
> **1887**

Below his name, a small relief of an anvil — the same shape as the medallion already in Jim's
pack.

He looks past the case, toward a doorway at the back of the room he hasn't noticed until now.

> **JIM:** *"...What's back there."*

## SCENE 18 — THE BLACK VEIN CAVERN

![The Black Vein Cavern — room concept](../Assets/Reference/foundry_black_vein_cavern_concept.png)

Past the Boardroom, a short passage where all human construction gradually stops. Reinforced
concrete gives way to older mine tunnel, then bare rock, then a threshold that isn't built at all.

*Interaction prompt: [ENTER CAVERN]*

Jim steps through. And stops.

Black Vein runs through the cavern walls themselves — massive, branching, ancient-looking
formations, dark and glistening faintly in whatever light reaches this deep.

> **JIM:** *"...Jesus."*

He doesn't approach it.

A small recorder sits on a flat stone near the threshold.

*Interaction prompt: [PLAY RECORDER]*

> **VOICE (RECORDING, FITCH OR A SURVIVING FOREMAN):** *"Anyone receiving this — stay away from
> the Foundry. Whatever Vanguard tells you, don't come down here."*

A long pause.

> **VOICE (RECORDING):** *"They didn't find this thing tonight. They've been digging at it for
> years."*

Static. Jim stands at the threshold a while longer before he finally turns back the way he came.
