## SCENE 1 — NORTHEAST DISTRICT ENTRY / APPROACH

Jim leaves whichever part of Ravenwood he was in behind. The buildings change again — wider
setbacks, ambulance-only lanes, a helipad windsock on a low rooftop hanging dead in the rain. A
faded blue-and-white sign, one bulb still working out of a dozen, reads **ST. DYMPHNA HOSPITAL —
EMERGENCY →.**

St. Dymphna comes into view a block out — large, multi-story, most windows dark. A few floors show
weak, inconsistent emergency lighting, flickering in a pattern that has nothing to do with any
storm.

A hospital PA system crackles on somewhere above him, intermittent, looping.

> **PA (RECORDED, DISTANT):** *"...if anyone can hear this... do not enter Emergency..."*

Static. Jim keeps walking. Closer now.

> **PA (RECORDED):** *"...avoid the lower level..."*

He's almost at the entrance when it cycles again, closer still, clearer.

> **PA (RECORDED):** *"...if you're police... we're still here..."*

Jim stops for a second. He's not police, and if the Police Station's already behind him, he knows
exactly how few of them are left to hear that.

> **JIM:** *"...Yeah. I know."*

He keeps walking toward the Ambulance Bay.

## SCENE 2 — THE AMBULANCE BAY

The Ambulance Bay is the hospital's version of the Police Station's wrecked patrol lot: overturned
gurneys, spent shell casings — some RPD, some a caliber Jim doesn't recognize — a crashed ambulance
nosed into a parked one, doors hanging open on both, abandoned medical equipment scattered across
the wet concrete. Blood trails run in more than one direction, crossing each other rather than
leading anywhere in particular.

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH EQUIPMENT]*

Near an overturned gurney, half-covered by a dropped equipment tray: a fire axe, department-red,
clearly hospital property rather than a firefighter's own.

*ITEM ACQUIRED: FIRE AXE*

> **JIM:** *"Everybody's got one of these lying around tonight."*

### If Jim has 0–1 emblems: Richard Dalton (alive)

A man bursts out of a side door near the bay, moving too fast for the footing, nearly goes down on
the wet concrete. **Richard Dalton** — Jim recognizes him immediately from the hotel lobby, though
neither of them says so right away. He doesn't look hurt. He looks like someone who's been running
on adrenaline for hours with nothing to spend it on.

> **RICHARD:** *"Hey — hey, are you— are you staff? Please tell me you're staff."*
>
> **JIM:** *"No. Richard, right? From the hotel."*

That stops him for half a second — long enough to actually look at Jim instead of past him.

> **RICHARD:** *"...Jim. You made it out."*
>
> **JIM:** *"Barely. What's going on?"*

Richard's composure, thin to begin with, starts slipping.

> **RICHARD:** *"Maria's in there. Maternity ward. I went to find a doctor because nobody's come by
> in— I don't even know how long, and I couldn't find anybody, everybody's either gone or dealing
> with something worse than us, and I don't— "*

He stops himself. Presses a hand to his own face for a second.

> **RICHARD:** *"I'm sorry. I know you just got here. I know this isn't your problem."*
>
> **JIM:** *"It's fine. Where is she exactly?"*
>
> **RICHARD:** *"Maternity. Third floor, or — I think third. I got turned around twice already."*

Jim looks past him, toward the dark building.

> **JIM:** *"I'll find her. Stay here — or somewhere safer than out in the open, at least."*

Richard nods too many times, the way people do when they're agreeing with something they haven't
actually processed.

> **RICHARD:** *"Okay. Okay. Just— come back and tell me. Whatever it is. Don't just not come
> back."*
>
> **JIM:** *"I will."*

He doesn't. Not yet — not until Jim's actually found her.

> *Design/story note: this is [Richard Dalton's](../Characters/Richard_Dalton.md) alive-version
> Tier 2b beat. Per his own file's explicit instruction, Jim's eventual "what do I tell him" return
> conversation is deliberately not written here — this scene ends on the promise, not the payoff.
> Richard survives the night regardless of what Jim finds in the Maternity Ward (Scene 13, below);
> only the tone of a future return scene depends on it, and that scene doesn't exist yet.*

### If Jim has 2+ emblems: Richard Dalton (dead)

No one comes out of the side door. Instead, near the edge of the bay, half-behind an overturned
gurney: a body, face-down, one arm still outstretched toward the hospital's side entrance he never
reached. Jim turns him over. Recognizes him immediately.

> **JIM:** *"...Richard."*

He doesn't say anything else for a moment. There's nothing on the body that explains exactly what
got to him, and Jim doesn't spend long looking for an answer.

> **JIM:** *"You were trying to get back in."*

He straightens up. Looks at the dark building ahead — somewhere in there, still unaware, is the
reason Richard was out here at all.

> **JIM:** *"...I'll find her anyway."*

> *Design/story note: the dead-version staging (near the Ambulance Bay, face-down, one arm
> outstretched toward the entrance) is a new decision — previously flagged as "not yet decided" in
> [`Characters/Richard_Dalton.md`](../Characters/Richard_Dalton.md) → "Unresolved Ideas." Per the
> survivor system's rule that no death is ever staged as the player's fault, nothing here implies
> Jim could have prevented this — he simply arrives too late, same as every other Tier 2/2b loss.*

## SCENE 3 — EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT / TRIAGE HALL (HUB)

Through the Ambulance Bay's inner doors: the Emergency Department, a wide hall lined with gurneys,
most still made up with clean sheets nobody got to use again. Hallway triage stations sit abandoned
mid-use — a blood-pressure cuff still wrapped around a mannequin arm on a training dummy nobody ever
put away, a vitals monitor cycling through a flatlined readout nobody's turned off.

Jim can see the shape of the district immediately, the same way he could at the Bullpen. Four
sealed points from here:

- **LABORATORY** — keyed lock.
- **ADMINISTRATION** — keyed lock.
- **SURGICAL WING** — a card-reader lock, dark, unresponsive to anything he's carrying yet.
- A stairwell up, barricaded from this side with a gurney and two overturned filing cabinets, a
  hand-lettered sign zip-tied to the barricade: **PSYCH WARD — DO NOT — THEY ARE STILL IN THERE.**

Two doorways stand open with no lock at all: one toward **RADIOLOGY**, one toward the **MATERNITY
WARD** corridor. A dim stairwell near the nurses' station leads down, unmarked, toward the
**MORGUE.**

> **JIM:** *"...Same shape as the station. Different building."*

A shambler in scrubs shuffles between two gurneys, unaware.

*Combat: Shambler.*

Once it's down, the ED goes quiet — quiet enough that Jim can hear the flatlined monitor still
cycling somewhere behind him.

## SCENE 4 — RADIOLOGY

Through the unlocked doorway: a CT suite, the machine's ring still faintly lit, a restraint gurney
in the middle of the room still buckled open — the straps snapped rather than unbuckled. A
wall-mounted radio on the counter is on, tuned low, cycling through dead frequencies.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE RADIO]*

For a moment it catches something — a burst of police dispatch chatter, badly distorted.

> **DISPATCH (RADIO, DISTORTED):** *"Unit Twelve, respond Ravenwood Memorial. Violent patient.
> Radiology."*

Static swallows the rest. Jim looks at the restraint gurney, then at the radio.

> **JIM:** *"...That's here. That's this room."*

Movement from behind the CT machine — slow at first, then very much not. What used to be the
restrained patient, fully turned now, comes around the machine fast.

dangerous than an ordinary shambler.*

belt.

*Interaction prompt: [TAKE LABORATORY KEY]*

## SCENE 5 — THE LABORATORY

The Laboratory Key opens a room of microscopes, sample racks, and a pathologist's workstation — a
monitor still on, displaying a half-finished document.

*Interaction prompt: [READ MONITOR]*

> *"...preliminary findings inconsistent with any known coagulation or dermatological pathology.
> Cellular activity does not cease following trauma; it accelerates. Necrosis appears to provoke
> rather than suppress cellular activity. Requesting authorization for tissue sampling outside
> Ravenwood — "*

The document ends there. Unsent, unauthorized, unfinished.

> **JIM:** *"...Cellular activity accelerates. That's not a disease. That's — "*

He doesn't finish the sentence either.

## SCENE 6 — ADMINISTRATION

The Administration Key opens a suite of offices, decor a level nicer than anywhere else in the
building — carpet instead of tile, framed accreditation certificates, a reception desk with a dead
phone system still lit up with a dozen unanswered lines blinking red.

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH FILES]*

### Document — Vanguard's V-CASE protocol for hospital staff

> Symptom checklist, near-identical to the Police Station's own V-CASE material. Procedure:
> **stabilize and isolate pending specialist transfer.** Explicitly barred: biopsies, advanced
> imaging without separate authorization, tissue samples sent outside Ravenwood.

> **JIM:** *"They told doctors not to actually look at what was wrong with people. That's the whole
> policy."*

### Document — the Biohazard Containment Order

> A printed remote-lockdown directive, timestamped mid-outbreak. Justification: **preventing
> contamination beyond Ravenwood.** No signature — issued through infrastructure Vanguard itself
> installed, not through hospital administration at all.

### Document — the administrator's correspondence, increasingly panicked

> A short thread of printed emails, the administrator's own replies getting shorter and less
> composed as the night goes on, ending on an unanswered message sent twenty minutes before the
> building's outgoing communications finally cut out entirely: *"You are locking doctors and
> patients inside a building with this. Please advise."* No reply logged.

### Optional — a physician's confused call transcript

*Interaction prompt: [READ TRANSCRIPT]*

> A handwritten transcript of an incoming call, the physician's own confusion audible in how it's
> written down:
>
> *"Who is this?"*
>
> *"We have records."*
>
> *"Cellular activity increases after tissue damage."*
>
> *"...Yes."*
>
> *"How the hell do you know that?"*
>
> *"Because you're not the first people to see it."*

Jim reads it twice.

> **JIM:** *"Not the first people to see it. Then who was?"*

### Optional — a one-line transmission relayed outward

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE RADIO LOG]*

> A logged outgoing message: *"Do not perform invasive treatment unless absolutely necessary."*
> Relayed, per a handwritten routing note, to **Worthy Academy** — passed on as emergency guidance
> to shelter staff with no medical training and no ability to act on it.

> **JIM:** *"...Sent that to a school."*

### Optional — early triage chart

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE CHART]*

> An early intake chart notes an unexplained cluster of industrial-trauma admissions, all listing
> the same employer: **Steelgate Refinery.** Nobody flagged it as connected to anything yet.

Jim sets everything back down exactly where he found it, out of habit more than any real reason to.

## SCENE 7 — THE MORGUE

A dim, unmarked stairwell off the nurses' station leads down into the morgue — no lock, easy to
miss. Cold, tiled, a wall of drawers, most closed. One gurney nearby holds a sheet pulled back over
nothing at all.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE RADIO LOG]*

A logbook lies open on the attendant's desk, the entries stopping mid-sentence:

> *"Bringing down another one from the ED. Getting crowded down here. Going to need — "*

Nothing after it. Beside it, a handheld radio, powered off.

*Interaction prompt: [PLAY LAST TRANSMISSION]*

> **ATTENDANT (RECORDING):** *"I need somebody down here. Now. One of these people isn't dead."*

Static. No response was ever recorded.

> **JIM:** *"...Somebody heard that. Nobody came."*

### Optional — the drawers

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH DRAWERS]*

Most are exactly what they should be. One isn't. It slides open on its own the moment Jim's hand
gets near the handle — a scare beat, not a real fight.

*Combat: one Shambler, staged for shock rather than difficulty.*

> **JIM:** *"Yeah. No. Staying shut."*

## SCENE 9 — THE SURGICAL WING

Back at the hospital, the access card wakes the card-reader with a soft chime — the same
out-of-place technology Jim already recognizes from the Police Station's Vanguard doors, though
here it's plausibly just ordinary hospital security rather than anything covert.

*Interaction prompt: [USE ACCESS CARD]*

The Surgical Wing beyond is worse than anything else in the building so far — an operating room
mid-procedure, abandoned exactly as it was left: surgical trays still laid out, instruments dropped
rather than set down, a ruined length of gauze trailing off the table onto the floor. A logbook sits
open on a rolling cart near the door.

*Interaction prompt: [READ SURGICAL LOG]*

> *"Abdominal trauma, extensive. Began standard exploratory procedure. Within minutes, tissue at the
> incision site began regenerating — malformed, disorganized, faster than we could work. We
> stopped the procedure because every incision produced additional growth. Patient did not
> arrest. Patient did not die on the table."*

The entry ends there. Jim looks up from the log, toward the deeper doors at the back of the wing —
the ones marked **OR 3.**

> **JIM:** *"Didn't die on the table. So where'd he go."*

He already knows the answer isn't going to be good.

## SCENE 10 — BOSS ENCOUNTER: "THE SURGEON"

OR 3 is dark, lit only by one working surgical lamp still swinging gently on its arm. Something
large is crouched in the far corner, back to Jim, hunched over what's left of an instrument tray.

*Interaction prompt: [ENTER OR 3]*

It turns. Aged, wrinkled human face. Glasses, somehow still on. A tattered coat hangs off one bare
shoulder. Where its other arm should be: an enormous, gnarled, clawed hand-limb, large enough that
the rest of its body sits on it rather than beside it — and Jim understands, watching it shift its
weight, that the huge hand isn't a growth it's carrying. It's what it walks on now.

> **JIM:** *"...Jesus."*

Its ordinary, still-human arm raises something small and metallic — a compact injector, more dart
gun than syringe.

*Combat: "The Surgeon" (see* [`Creatures/Unnamed_Hospital_Boss.md`](../Creatures/Unnamed_Hospital_Boss.md)*).
The giant hand doubles as both locomotion and its primary close-range attack — a ground-slam/lunge,
telegraphed by the creature rearing back and driving the hand down to propel itself forward. The
tranquilizer-gun arm is a separate, ranged threat, forcing Jim to manage distance against a boss
that closes ground in bursts rather than a steady approach. Full attack timing/phases not yet
mechanically finalized — see that file's own "Open Design Gaps."*

Once it's down, it collapses across its own giant hand, finally still. Jim doesn't look at its face
again longer than he has to.

> **JIM:** *"They called you something, back when you still had a name."*

He doesn't know what. Nobody wrote it down anywhere he's found yet.

## SCENE 11 — THE ICU

Past OR 3, a connecting corridor leads into the ICU — and the room tells its own story before Jim
reads a single note. Half the ward sits in total darkness, monitors dead, ventilators silent. The
other half hums quietly along on backup power, lights steady, machines running exactly as they
should.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE POWER SPLIT]*

> **JIM:** *"...Half the room has power. Half doesn't. That's not a power failure. That's a
> choice."*

In the lit half: one bed, one working ventilator, and two bodies — a patient, and beside him, a
nurse who clearly didn't leave when she could have.

*Interaction prompt: [READ CHART]*

Clipped to the end of the bed, a handwritten note in two different hands.

> *"Mr. Palmer is ventilator dependent. We cannot move him without power."*

Underneath, a different hand:

> *"I'll stay."*

Jim looks at both of them a moment longer than the room asks for.

> **JIM:** *"...Yeah. I figured."*

## SCENE 12 — ST. DYMPHNA CHAPEL

The Chapel Key opens a small, hushed room, entirely unlike anywhere else in the hospital — a dozen
pews, a modest stained-glass window filtering weak gray daylight-that-isn't into colored fragments
across the floor. A portrait hangs beside a small brass founding plaque: a formally dressed
19th-century physician, stethoscope draped with obvious period-appropriate pride rather than
practicality.

> **DR. NATHANIEL VOSS**
> **FOUNDING PHYSICIAN**
> **1887**

Beneath his name, a small relief of a serpent coiled around a staff.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE CASE]*

A glass-fronted display case beside the portrait holds a bronze, wedge-shaped medallion — the same
shape Jim's already carrying one of.

> **JIM:** *"...There you are."*

*Interaction prompt: [OPEN CASE]*

Unlike the Old Station House's brittle antique glass, this case has an actual working latch —
someone here still dusted it, once. It opens without needing to be broken.

*ITEM ACQUIRED: MEDICAL CREST*

> **JIM:** *"Dr. Nathaniel Voss. Founding physician."*

He looks back at the empty pews behind him.

> **JIM:** *"Hope whatever you built held up better than the building did."*

## SCENE 13 — THE MATERNITY WARD

Off the Emergency Department, deliberately unlocked — no key, no lock, nothing gating this room at
all. Jim can walk in the moment he chooses to. The hallway leading in is quiet in a way that feels
wrong given everything else in the building.

Inside: a private room, one bed. **Maria Dalton** — Jim recognizes her instantly, the same way he
recognized Richard. She's clearly just given birth; the scene reads as finished rather than in
progress, morbid by what it implies rather than by anything shown directly. She didn't survive it.

> **JIM:** *"...Maria."*

He stands there for a moment. Doesn't touch anything yet.

> **JIM:** *"Richard's outside. He doesn't know."*

*(This line only plays if Richard is alive per Scene 2's conditional branch; if Richard is already
dead, Jim says nothing here — there's no one left to not-know.)*

Movement, low and fast, from beneath the far side of the bed — something small, hunched, moving on
clawed hands as much as legs.

*Combat: The Broodling (see* [`Creatures/Broodling.md`](../Creatures/Broodling.md)*) — small
profile, fast and low, scrabbling rather than walking. Deliberately hard to hit reliably; proposed
as a precision fight rather than a damage check. Full combat kit not yet mechanically finalized —
see that file's own "Open Design Gaps."*

Once it's down, Jim doesn't stay in the room longer than he has to.

> **JIM:** *"...I'm sorry."*

He says it to her, not to what he just fought.

> *Design/story note: scripts* [`Characters/Maria_Dalton.md`](../Characters/Maria_Dalton.md) *and*
> [`Creatures/Broodling.md`](../Creatures/Broodling.md) *exactly as those files specify — morbid by

## SCENE 16 — THE PSYCHIATRIC / BEHAVIORAL HEALTH WARD

Back at the Emergency Department, the pry bar forces the barricaded stairwell — the gurney and
filing cabinets shriek across the floor as Jim clears them aside.

*Interaction prompt: [FORCE BARRICADE]*

> **JIM:** *"'They are still in there.' Great."*

Up the stairwell: a nurses' station and a row of locked cells, several standing open, one bent
outward rather than unlocked. Documents are scattered across the nurses' station counter — patient
files, several stamped with the same **"V-CASE TRANSFERRED"** marking Jim may already recognize
from Detective Cole's own investigation at the Police Station.

*Interaction prompt: [READ FILES]*

> Names, dates, and transfer stamps matching the Police Station's own confidential watchlist system
> almost exactly — proof the two institutions were feeding the same program from opposite ends of
> the city.

> **JIM:** *"Same stamp. Same program. Different building."*

Shapes move between the cells — more than one.

*Combat: multiple Shamblers in a tight nurses'-station-and-cells layout — the district's most
dangerous optional encounter, deliberately staged as a war of attrition in close quarters rather
than a single tough fight.*

Once the ward is clear, Jim doesn't linger.

> *Design/story note: scripts* [`Locations/Hospital.md`](../Locations/Hospital.md) *→ "Storyline"
> and "Documents" — the Psychiatric Ward's V-CASE transfer records, the strongest direct crossover
> document between the Hospital and the Police Station's own Vanguard sub-plot.*

## SCENE 17 — THE HOSPITAL PARKING STRUCTURE

A multi-level parking structure adjoining the hospital, mostly abandoned cars, a directory sign for
levels that don't matter anymore. Quiet in the specific way of a place people ran through rather
than to.

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH LEVEL]*

One car near the ramp has its windows covered from the inside with taped-up cardboard and a
blanket — someone barricaded themselves in rather than trying for the hospital doors.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE VEHICLE]*

Jim looks through a gap in the cardboard. Doesn't need to open the door to know.

> **JIM:** *"...You held out longer than the door would've."*

He doesn't open it. Whoever they were, there's no name here to attach to it, and Jim doesn't go
looking for one.

### Optional — supply cart

*Interaction prompt: [SEARCH SUPPLY CART]*

*ITEM AVAILABLE (optional): MEDKIT, plus HANDGUN AMMUNITION.*

A rear exit ramp, gate chain-cut and standing open, leads directly onto the surrounding street
grid.

*Navigation shortcut: this ramp provides a direct route between the Parking Structure and the wider
district street grid, bypassing the main hospital approach on return trips.*
