> match [`Locations/Police_Station.md`](../Locations/Police_Station.md)'s **Lockdown Puzzle** —
> see [`CANON.md`](../CANON.md) → "Five Puzzle Philosophies." The previous version of this script
> (a straightforward Reyes-has-the-armory-key style chain) is fully superseded; full rationale for
> the redesign is in [`STORY_NOTES.md`](../STORY_NOTES.md).
>
> Picks up immediately after [`Chapter_2_Ravenwood.md`](Chapter_2_Ravenwood.md), Scene 21 (City
> Hall) — Jim has just left Downtown and entered the Southwest civic complex. Covers the full
> Police Station critical path, all three secondary locations (Fire Station, Municipal Garage,
> City Courthouse), and the optional Vanguard sub-plot.
>
> **Jim's arc note:** per [`Characters/Jim_Mercer.md`](../Characters/Jim_Mercer.md), if this is

> questions, not making accusations. Dialogue throughout this script should read that way, and
> should generally say *less*, not more, than the previous version — cutting reactions that just
> restate a document the player already read.

## SCENE 1 — SOUTHWEST DISTRICT ENTRY

Jim leaves the downtown grid behind. The buildings change character within a few blocks —
storefronts give way to municipal architecture, wider streets, chain-link fencing, sodium lights
on tall poles (most dead, a few still flickering on backup power). A sign, half-knocked from its
post, still points the way: **CIVIC COMPLEX — POLICE — FIRE — COURTS — 2 BLOCKS.**

A row of patrol cars sits abandoned along the curb, doors open, overhead lights long since drained
of charge. A dropped riot shield lies face-down in a puddle, cracked straight through.

*SFX: rain on chain-link and sheet metal; a radio antenna somewhere clicking rhythmically against
a pole in the wind; distant, occasional gunfire — old, not current, more echo than event.*

### Crest-count branch (per [`Characters/Jim_Mercer.md`](../Characters/Jim_Mercer.md))

**0 crests (Survival):**

> **JIM:** *"...Should've been the safest part of town."*

**1–2 crests (Suspicion / Pattern):**

> **JIM:** *"...Let's see what this one's version looks like."*

**3+ crests (Anger and past it):**

Jim doesn't say anything. He just keeps walking toward the building.

## SCENE 2 — RAVENWOOD POLICE STATION — EXTERIOR

The station comes into view at the end of the block: a squat two-story municipal building, modern
brick and glass, **RAVENWOOD POLICE DEPARTMENT** in raised steel letters above the entrance —
several letters dark, the sign lit unevenly. Attached to its west side, separated by a narrow
breezeway, sits something much older: a squared-off stone building with an arched entrance and a
weathered cornerstone. A small brass plaque beside its own boarded door reads **ORIGINAL STATION
HOUSE — EST. 1887 — RAVENWOOD HISTORICAL REGISTER.**

The parking lot is a wreck of its own making: cruisers nosed into each other, a folding barricade
table on its side, sawhorses scattered rather than stacked. One cruiser's light bar still turns,
red and blue, uselessly, over an empty lot.

> **JIM:** *"Front door's the modern half. Old building's boarded up tight."*

He looks at the boarded stone building a moment longer than he means to — the note from the
guardhouse still sitting in his mind. *Look at the police station. The old building. Not the
annex.*

*Interaction prompt: [APPROACH MAIN ENTRANCE]*

## SCENE 3 — STATION LOBBY

![Station Lobby — room concept](../Assets/Reference/police_station_lobby_concept.png)

The lobby doors are propped open with a fire extinguisher. Inside: a bulletproof reception window,
spiderwebbed with a single hard impact but not broken through; a row of plastic waiting-room
chairs, one overturned; a bulletin board thick with lost-dog flyers and a faded D.A.R.E. poster. A
hand-lettered sign taped to the reception window reads: **RING BELL FOR SERVICE.** The bell sits
on the counter, undisturbed.

Somewhere deeper in the building, muffled by walls and distance, static crackles — a radio scanner
still running on backup power, cycling through dead channels.

A shape shifts behind the reception window — a former officer, still in uniform, rises from where
it had slumped against the counter.

*Combat: Shambler (police uniform) — safely behind reinforced glass, not a real threat.*

It can't reach him through the window. Jim moves past toward the door marked **BULLPEN —
AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL.**

## SCENE 4 — THE BULLPEN

![The Bullpen — room concept](../Assets/Reference/police_station_bullpen_concept.png)

The bullpen is a single open room — a dozen desks in rows, computer monitors dark, a tactical map
of Ravenwood pinned up near the door. Paperwork is scattered everywhere. A coffee station in the
corner still has a full pot, long gone cold. Two desks near the window have been shoved together
and turned on their sides — a hasty barricade, built and then abandoned.

A wall-mounted console dominates one side of the room — a station map board, a bank of indicator
lights, and a small screen still lit on backup power. Jim gets close enough to read it.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE SECURITY CONSOLE]*

```
EMERGENCY LOCKDOWN ACTIVE
COMMAND AUTHORIZATION INCOMPLETE
SECURE AREAS: 4
```

Four zone indicators glow red beneath the readout, unlabeled beyond a room number each: nothing
Jim recognizes yet.

> **JIM:** *"...Four."*

He tries the console's manual override. Nothing — it wants an authorization sequence he doesn't
have.

> **JIM:** *"Not going to just turn off, is it."*

A door tucked past the tactical map bears a sleek corporate placard — **VANGUARD PUBLIC SAFETY
LIAISON** — out of place against every hand-lettered sign in the room. It doesn't budge when he
tries it, and it's not one of the console's four zones either.

> **JIM:** *"...What's that doing in a police station?"*

A smaller door nearby, propped open, has a nameplate reading **DET. A. COLE.** Unlike everywhere
else in the bullpen, nothing past that door looks disturbed.

A shambler — a heavyset officer, vest still buckled — rises from behind one of the overturned
desks.

*Combat: Shambler (police uniform).*

Once it's down, a voice cuts through the quiet from behind the Records/Dispatch door.

> **VOICE (CALLOWAY):** *"If you're gonna make that much noise, you'd better not be one of them."*

> **JIM:** *"...I'm not."*

> **VOICE (CALLOWAY):** *"Everybody says that. Door's not locked, it's barricaded. Give me a
> minute."*

### Optional — the whiteboard

A whiteboard on wheels sits in the middle of the room, three columns ruled across it in marker,
the handwriting less careful the further down each list goes.

*Interaction prompt: [EXAMINE WHITEBOARD]*

> T. OKONKWO — PATROL

> **MISSING**

>
> **DECEASED**
> UNIT 4
> UNIT 9 (PARTIAL)

Pinned crooked over the corner of the DECEASED column, a printed sheet.

*Interaction prompt: [READ DIRECTIVE]*

> **EMERGENCY PUBLIC SAFETY DIRECTIVE 7**
>
> ISSUED BY: VANGUARD INDUSTRIES — OFFICE OF PUBLIC SAFETY COORDINATION
>
> EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY, ALL RAVENWOOD PD PERSONNEL WILL:
> 1. ESTABLISH ROADBLOCKS AT ALL PRIMARY EGRESS POINTS FROM RAVENWOOD MUNICIPAL LIMITS.
> 2. PREVENT CIVILIAN FOOT AND VEHICLE TRAFFIC FROM LEAVING RAVENWOOD UNTIL FURTHER NOTICE.
> 3. RESTRICT ALL ACCESS TO VANGUARD-DESIGNATED FACILITIES.
> 4. DETAIN ANY INDIVIDUAL EXHIBITING CONTAMINATION SYMPTOMS PER STANDING V-CASE PROTOCOL.
> 5. MAINTAIN RADIO SILENCE REGARDING THE INDUSTRIAL INCIDENT ON ALL OPEN CHANNELS.

Jim lowers the sheet without saying anything.

## SCENE 5 — SERGEANT CALLOWAY

![Records/Dispatch — room concept](../Assets/Reference/police_station_dispatch_concept.png)

*This scene plays if the Police Station is the first district Jim visits. See the design note at
the end of this scene for what happens if it isn't.*

The door scrapes open against a filing cabinet dragged clear from the other side. **Sergeant Ruth
Calloway** stands in the gap — mid-fifties, uniform still buttoned correctly, gray hair pulled
back tight, a fire axe held competently in both hands.

> **CALLOWAY:** *"You're not one of mine and you're not one of them. That's a good start."*
>
> **JIM:** *"Jim Mercer. I was staying at the hotel."*
>
> **CALLOWAY:** *"The hotel's still standing?"*
>
> **JIM:** *"Barely."*

She waves him into the small dispatch room — dead monitors, a desk radio still drawing power, a
cot slept on in short shifts rather than real sleep.

> **CALLOWAY:** *"Ruth Calloway. Desk sergeant. Radio's dead outside the building — county, state,
> all of it, gone. One working channel, and all it does is repeat the Deadlock order back at me."*
>
> **JIM:** *"I saw the checkpoint. County line's sealed."*
>
> **CALLOWAY:** *"Sealed *at* the county line. We're a long way from the county line, Mr. Mercer."*

> **JIM:** *"There's a statue in the park. Slots for something — five of them. Groundskeeper said a
> historian told him to look at the old station house."*

Calloway's expression shifts — something finally making sense after sitting wrong for years.

> **CALLOWAY:** *"The old key thing. Display case, main hall, next door. Every officer who's ever
> worked out of this building has seen it. First marshal's name's on it. Hale, I think."*
>
> **JIM:** *"Nobody ever asked why it's shaped like that?"*
>
> **CALLOWAY:** *"People stopped asking questions about this town's antiques a long time before I
> got here."*

She works a key off a hook behind the desk.

> **CALLOWAY:** *"Chief's key. His office is off the bullpen. If anyone would know what that key
> thing is for, it's him — and the old building's own key has been his responsibility as long as
> I've worked here."*

*ITEM ACQUIRED: CHIEF'S OFFICE KEY*

> **JIM:** *"What happened to the front door? The console out there says four sections are locked
> down."*

Calloway's jaw tightens slightly.

> **CALLOWAY:** *"Started sometime after midnight. Booking first — someone we'd brought in stopped
> being someone we could help. After that it's a blur to me, but the system doesn't forget. Whoever
> triggered each one had to do it from wherever they were standing, which means the order's
> probably written down somewhere, if you're patient enough to look for it. Chief kept a log."*
>
> **JIM:** *"And the last one?"*
>
> **CALLOWAY:** *"Corporal Reyes went to check on Diesel and Baxter around midnight when they
> wouldn't stop barking. He never came back. I don't know if he's the one who finished sealing the
> old station, or if the Chief did it himself once Reyes stopped answering. Kennel room's down the
> back hallway past the break room. I'd go myself, but somebody has to hold this room."*

A beat.

> **CALLOWAY:** *"There's a padlock on the Property Room, too, separate from whatever the console's
> doing. Bolt cutters would handle it, if you care. Fire station's got some, two blocks over. Not
> going to pretend it's the important part of your night."*

### Optional dialogue — Detective Cole's office

*Available if Jim asks about the undisturbed office off the bullpen.*

> **JIM:** *"There's a detective's office out there. Looks untouched."*

Calloway's expression goes carefully flat.

> **CALLOWAY:** *"Cole. Good detective. Got walked out over an Internal Affairs mess I never
> believed for a second."*
>
> **JIM:** *"You think there was more to it."*
>
> **CALLOWAY:** *"I think a lot of things tonight, Mr. Mercer. I try not to finish most of those
> sentences."*

She picks the fire axe back up off the desk — not threatening, just needing something in her
hands. Nobody's cleared his desk. Nobody wanted to be the one.

### Optional — the dispatch recorder

Wired into the console: a reel-to-reel emergency logger, red light steady.

*Interaction prompt: [PLAY RECORDER]*

Calloway doesn't stop him.

> **LOG — EXCHANGE WITH ST. DYMPHNA HOSPITAL:**
>
> *"RPD, are you receiving?" / "Memorial, go ahead." / "We have forty-plus civilians and can't move
> them." / "We're trying to open Highway 13."* (long static) *"RPD?"* (static) *"Ravenwood Police,
> please respond."* Nothing.

Another entry, earlier, badly degraded — half the words lost to static rather than a clean
recording.

> **LOG — UNKNOWN SOURCE, PARTIALLY LOST:**
>
> *"...not a chemical..."* (static) *"...Vanguard knew..."* (static) *"...mine is the..."*

Static swallows the rest. No callsign, no timestamp anyone bothered writing down. Jim listens to
it twice. It doesn't get any clearer the second time.

> **JIM:** *"...Whoever that was, I hope somebody else heard the rest of it."*

A short gap of tape hiss. Then the Chief's final broadcast.

> **LOG — CHIEF'S FINAL BROADCAST, ALL FREQUENCIES:**
>
> *"This is Chief Marcus Doyle, Ravenwood Police Department, broadcasting on all emergency
> frequencies."* (static) *"All surviving officers, disregard Vanguard containment orders,
> effective immediately. Help civilians evacuate by any route still open to you. Do not surrender
> injured persons to Vanguard personnel for any reason."* (a long pause) *"Whatever they told us
> this was — they lied. I don't know what it actually is. I know it isn't what they said."*
> (gunfire, close) *"— hold the —"* (static)

The recording ends there. Jim reaches over and switches the logger off himself.

> **JIM:** *"...Chief."*
